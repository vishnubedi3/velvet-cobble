Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-02 (all eras), MIL-01 (§7), DYN-01 (§5); constrains TIM-03+, the later MIL/DYN files
Why it matters: the specific commanders (the Shreshthas, the frontier generals, the military houses — the individual commanders, their KE dates, their names) are the agents of the military events (the frontiers, the Vethra wars, the Khor campaigns, the Sareth defense, the delta defense) — the Shreshtha's dominance (KE ~480–500) is the precondition for the Great Succession Crisis.
