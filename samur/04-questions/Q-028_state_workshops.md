# Q-028 The state workshops (the karkhanas)

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ECO-01 (§6); constrains TEC-01 (the industrialization, the metallurgy)
Why it matters: the specific state workshops (the karkhanas — the silk, the steel, the textiles, the paper — the scale, the labor, the military demand) define the industrialization and the military demand (the steel, the guns for the frontier) — the dual-demand industrialization (the military + the court) is a load-bearing feature of the Samur economy (ECO-01 §6).
Current best guesses: **state workshops** (the karkhanas) in the major cities (Kesra, Besra, Voren) producing the **silk, the steel, the textiles, the paper** — the **military demand** (the steel, the guns for the frontier) + the **court demand** (the silk, the textiles) drive the **metallurgy and the crafts** — the **scale** and the **labor** (the craft guilds, the corvée) are to be fixed (TEC-01).
