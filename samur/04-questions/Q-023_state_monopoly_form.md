# Q-023 The specific form of the state monopoly

Status: RESOLVED → ECO-02 §2 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the specific form of the state monopoly (which are monopolized, which are taxed)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the ECO-01 §3 fixed the "state monopoly" but not the form (monopolized vs. taxed).
- Evidence/reasoning: the Mughal state monopoly (the salt/horse/pepper) + the Maratha shahukar (the licensed middlemen) + the GEO-01 §5 resource scarcity.
- Alternatives considered: a total state monopoly (rejected — the textiles are the guilds' domain, the ECO-01 §6); no monopoly (rejected — the GEO-01 §5 scarcity needs the state control).
- Selected resolution: the **licensed monopoly** — the state controls the source (the salt pans, the Khor horse import, the Veth pepper via the Kesveth purchase), sells via the licensed middlemen (the shahukar holds the licenses); the salt = the universal (every village); the horse = the military (the cavalry + the licensed horse-dealers at Keshkhor); the pepper = the trade (the state buys at Kesveth/Voren, sells at the court/export — the largest profit, the most contested; the Phre challenges it from KE 500); the textiles = taxed, not monopolized (the guilds produce; the state takes the transit dues + the court purchase).
- Why chosen: it is the Mughal state monopoly + the Maratha shahukar made nominally operationally operational, with the Phre challenge as the TIM-03 delta friction.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the ECO-02 §2, the TIM-03 (the Phre challenge, the KE 500), the MIL-02 §2 (the horse monopoly).
- Canon status: CANON (ECO-02 §2).
