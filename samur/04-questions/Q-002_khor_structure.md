# Q-002 The Khor's size and internal structure

Status: RESOLVED → FOR-01 §3.1 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01 (§5, §6); FOR-01 (§3); DYN-01 (the frontier policy), MIL-01 (the hard frontier), ECO-01 (the horse/salt/silver trade)
Why it mattered: whether the Khor is one confederation or several horse-people changes the empire's entire northern strategy, the horse price, and the shape of the northern military response.
**Resolution (now canon, FOR-01 §3.1):** the Khor is **multiple horse peoples** (the steppe) with a **loose seasonal assembly** (not a single confederation, but multiple competing horse peoples). The **seasonal assembly** (the trade, the raiding, the marriage) is the political structure. The **specific structure** (the number, the names, the leaders) is Q-047.
