# Q-041 The specific personal names

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-02 §1 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: CUL-01 (§6); the later CUL/narrative files; DYN-02 (§1)
Why it mattered: the specific personal names (the kings, the Shreshthas, the commanders, the merchants, the priests) are the canon name pool for the later narrative phase (if authorized).
**Resolution (now canon, DYN-02 §1):** the personal-name pool is the **consonant-rich** Samur-language register (the CUL-01 toponymy), three classes — **the place-name doubling** (Kesra, Veshna, Besra, Threna, Gheshar, Voren, Kesveth, Keshkhor, Tarn, Sareth, Khel, Oren), **the royal/elite -an/-ar names** (Sharan, Dharan, Vethan, Kharan, Varan, Peshan, Ghoran, Zharan, Maren, Neshan, Keshan, Taren, Orenar, Sarethan), **the -esh/-ath names** (Tarnesh, Khesh, Rhaesh, Dhresh, Kesath, Veshath, Threnath). The **name-pool rule:** all personal names must come from this pool; no invented names without a pool entry.
