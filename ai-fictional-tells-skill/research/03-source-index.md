## Notes

- S17/S18/S19 concern academic writing; the transfer to fiction is via the **RLHF
  mechanism** (K8), not via the word lists themselves. The taxonomy contains no
  fiction "AI word list."
- S28 and S15/S27 appear to conflict with S02/S04. They do not: reader judgment and
  distributional statistics measure different things. The skill's objective is literary
  quality (S04's expert criteria), not reader-side indistinguishability (S28).
- Sources dated 2025–2026 reflect the research frontier at the time of writing; the
  evidence hierarchy requires re-checking Low/Medium-confidence tells against new
  fiction-specific corpora as they appear (`../spec/09-evaluation-benchmark.md`).
