# Q-012 The founding Shreshtha

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-01 (§3, §7); will constrain DYN-02 (the specific kings), the King vs. Shreshtha fault line
Why it matters: the first holder of the prime-ministerial/regent office sets the precedent for the office's power and its relationship to the crown — a cousin, a general, or a temple-backed figure each implies a different balance of the dual structure.
Current best guesses: a **senior general of the Samur warrior band** (a fellow founding-family man, not of the royal house) — this keeps the office *outside* the royal house (so the dual structure is real) while making it *founding* (not a later appointment); his family becomes the first of the "military houses" (the founding elite).
