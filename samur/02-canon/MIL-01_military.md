> MIL-01 treats the military as a **political and economic institution** (per the brief) — **not** just a fighting force. The military is the state's **chief instrument of power**, its **chief employer**, and its **chief driver of the economy** (the war finance, the military demand, ECO-01 §5–6). The specific wars, battles, and commanders are TIM-02+ and the later MIL files.

## 1. The military as a political and economic institution

- The army is **not just a fighting force** — it is the **chief instrument of the state's power**, the **chief employer**, and the **chief driver of the economy** (the war finance, the military demand, ECO-01 §5–6).
- The army is **tied to the fiscal-military system** (the DEM-01 geometry: a small garrisoned border defending a large dense core).
- The army is a **political institution** (the dual elite: the founding elite holds the top military offices, the absorbed elite holds the lower offices; the **King vs. the Shreshtha** fault line runs through the army, DYN-01 §3).

## 2. Recruitment (the layered structure)

1. **The peasant-soldier base** (the majority) — recruited from the **dense agrarian core** (the bār + the pell, DEM-01 §5 — the ~70–80% of the population in the agrarian zones). The **martial castes** (the kshatriya / the agrarian-military castes) are the primary source (the Sanatan varna/jāti logic).
2. **The chiefs' levies** — the absorbed nayaka provide the **district levies** (the military-service obligation, DYN-01 §2, ADM-01 §2). The chiefs provide the men, the state provides the command.
3. **The border elite** — the **garrison** (the Sareth passes, the Khor frontier), the **horse-people** (the Khor cavalry — the Samur riders on the Khor horses), the **coastal marines** (the delta, Voren).
4. **The founding elite (the military houses)** — the top commanders (the Military Head, the frontier generals) are from the **founding Samur warrior band** (the military houses, DYN-01 §5, Q-012).

## 3. The arms of the army

- **The infantry** — the **base** (the peasant-soldiers, the bār/pell) — the majority.
- **The cavalry** — the **Khor horses** (the strategic dependency, GEO-01 §5, ECO-01 §3) — the **mobile striking force** — but it **depends on the Khor** (the horse trade) — a **strategic vulnerability**.
- **The war elephants** — from the **Sareth forests + the southern highlands** (the Samur signature, the Vijayanagara logic) — a **specialty** force (the ecology is limited).
- **The artillery** — from the **state workshops** (the steel, the guns, ECO-01 §6) — the **modernizing** force (the Mysore/Vijayanagara artillery logic) — the **military demand** drives the metallurgy.
- **The irregulars** (the gupta/dash, the Maratha logic) — the **raiding, foraging, harassing** force (the Khor frontier, the Tarn east) — **cannot be stood down** (the Maratha problem).
- **The marines** — the **delta, Voren** — the **coastal** force (the maritime, the Phre).

## 4. Logistics

- **The season** (GEO-01 §7) — the **dry-season campaign** (the firm ground, the steppe forage) + the **wet-season river logistics** (the high water, the sea-wind). The **military calendar** is set by the wind (TIM-01, the wind-anchored calendar).
- **The Oren** (the spine) — the **river logistics** (the grain, the troops, the supplies move down the Oren) — the **logistics spine** (GEO-01 §2).
- **The Khor horse dependency** — the **cavalry depends on the Khor** (the horse trade) — a **strategic vulnerability** (a failed Khor = no horses = no cavalry).
- **The mule/ox logistics** (the Sareth passes, the Tarn east) — the **land logistics** (the mules, the ox-carts) — the **costly** projection (the Sareth, the Tarn).
- **The supply line** — the **long supply line** is the **vulnerability** (the Maratha Panipat logic — the supply-line failure).

## 5. War finance

- **The shahukar (the merchant-banker)** — **finances the war** (the debt-financed war, the Maratha logic) — the **war finance is separate from the land revenue** → enables campaigns beyond the home base.
- **The growth imperative** — the **debt-financed war must keep conquering** (the captured revenue repays the loan) → the empire is **structurally pulled toward expansion** (ECO-01 §5, DYN-01 §5, TIM-01).
- **The revenue surplus** — the **fiscal base** (the bār grain, the silver) — the **state's own war finance** (the Mughal logic — the revenue surplus + the state trade).
- **The state monopoly** (the salt, the horses, the pepper) — the **war finance** (the state's commercial revenue).

The war finance is a **mix** of the **revenue surplus** (the state's own) + the **shahukar debt** (the merchant-banker) + the **state monopoly** (the commercial revenue). The **shahukar debt** creates the **growth imperative**.

## 6. The specialist forces (by border)

- **The Sareth pass garrisons** (Threna, Gheshar, the Ghul Gate) — the **western defense** (the chokepoints, the wall) — the **garrison** force (the Qing fuheng logic — the frontier generals with wide autonomy).
- **The Khor frontier cavalry** (Keshkhor) — the **northern defense + the horse trade** — the **cavalry** + the **irregulars** (the gupta/dash, the raiding) — the **hard frontier** (defensible only by presence).
- **The delta marines** (Voren) — the **coastal defense** (the maritime, the Phre) — the **marines** + the **riverine/sea force**.
- **The Tarn tributary force** (the chiefs' levies) — the **eastern** (the tribute, the local elites) — the **chiefs' levies** (the district force).
- **The core field army** (the bār core) — the **strike** force (the cavalry + the infantry + the artillery + the elephants) — the **mobile** force (the campaigns).

## 7. The military as a political institution

- **The dual elite** (DYN-01 §5) — the **top commanders** (the Military Head, the frontier generals) are from the **founding elite** (the military houses); the **lower officers** are from the **absorbed elite** (the chiefs, the clerks).
- **The King vs. the Shreshtha** fault line (DYN-01 §3) — the **army is the Shreshtha's power base** (the Shreshtha controls the army) — the **legitimacy (the King) vs. the power (the Shreshtha + the army)** fault line. When a **weak king** rises, the **Shreshtha dominates** (the Maratha Peshwa logic).
- **The chiefs' levies** — the **regional power base** (the chiefs provide the men) — the **chief's military power** is the **successor-state incubator** (the fragmentation).
- **The military houses** (the founding elite) — the **top commanders** — the **founding elite's military power** is the **dynasty's power base** (but it fragilizes, the Qing banner decay).

## 8. The long-term consequences of expansion / overstretch

- **The three-front condition** (GEO-01 §8.1) — the empire must hold the Sareth + the Khor + the coast simultaneously — the **standing overstretch** (the cost of holding > the revenue of holding).
- **The fiscal-military tension** (DEM-01 §2) — a **small, expensive garrisoned border** defending a **large, dense, taxable core** — the **garrison is small** (the border is thin) and the **core is cyclical** (the labor supply).
- **The growth imperative** (ECO-01 §5) — the **debt-financed war must keep conquering** — the **overstretch** (the empire is pulled toward expansion).
- **The conquest without integration** (the Mughal Deccan logic) — the **conquest without the capacity to absorb** (the new provinces produce revenue that cannot be absorbed by the fiscal system) → the **fiscal-military death spiral**.
- **The irregulars that cannot be stood down** (the Maratha logic) — the **gupta/dash** (the raiding force) — after a defeat, the irregulars **cannot be demobilized** (a standing problem).
- **The supply-line failure** (the Maratha Panipat logic) — the **long supply line** is the **vulnerability** (the overextension of the campaign).

These are all **root causes** of the Phase 5 decline (TIM-01).

## Consequences (immediate / unintended / long-term)

- **Immediate:** the military as a political/economic institution; the layered recruitment (the peasant base, the chiefs' levies, the border elite, the military houses); the arms (the infantry, the cavalry, the elephants, the artillery, the irregulars, the marines); the logistics (the season, the Oren, the Khor dependency, the mule/ox, the supply line); the war finance (the shahukar, the growth imperative, the revenue surplus, the state monopoly); the specialist forces (the Sareth garrison, the Khor cavalry, the delta marines, the Tarn levies, the core field army); the military as a political institution (the dual elite, the King vs. the Shreshtha, the chiefs' levies, the military houses).
- **Unintended:** the **Khor horse dependency** (a strategic vulnerability — a failed Khor = no cavalry); the **growth imperative** (the debt-financed war must keep conquering — the overstretch); the **irregulars that cannot be stood down** (the standing problem); the **supply-line failure** (the vulnerability); the **conquest without integration** (the fiscal-military death spiral).
- **Long-term:** the **three-front overstretch**, the **fiscal-military tension**, the **growth imperative**, the **conquest without integration**, the **irregulars that cannot be stood down**, the **supply-line failure** — all **root causes** of the Phase 5 decline (TIM-01).
