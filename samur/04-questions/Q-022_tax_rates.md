# Q-022 Specific tax rates

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ADM-01 (§3); constrains ECO-01 (the revenue), the monetization chain
Why it matters: the specific rates (the bār grain rate, the pell rate, the transit/market dues, the tribute rate) set the fiscal engine and the peasant's burden — the *inflation* of these rates over time is what breaks the monetization chain (the revolt, TIM-01).
Current best guesses: a **bār grain rate of ~1/4 to 1/3** of the yield (a plausible pre-industrial state share, the zabt logic); a **pell rate** similar; **transit/market dues** of a few % (the port, the state monopoly); the **tribute rate** (the Tarn) of ~1/4 (the chauth logic) — with the **rate held roughly steady in the High Empire** and **inflation (the real burden rising) in the Stress** (the targets inflate, TIM-01).
