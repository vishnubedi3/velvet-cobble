# Q-027 Trade volumes and routes

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ECO-01 (§3); constrains the later ECO files, FOR-01 (the trade)
Why it matters: the specific trade volumes and routes (the Oren, the passes, the Khor, the delta — the goods, the quantities, the partners) define the economic engine and the state's revenue — the trade wealth is at the coast (the delta, the Veth), and the Phre's capture of Voren (the maritime hub) is the economic trigger of the fragmentation (TIM-01).
Current best guesses: the **Oren** (the internal artery — the grain, the bulk goods), the **Sareth passes** (the western trade — the iron/timber in, the bār/textiles out), the **Khor horse trade** (the northern trade — the horses in, the silver/salt/bār out), the **delta/pepper (Voren)** (the maritime trade — the pepper/textiles/salt out, the silver/foreign goods in), the **Veth coast** (the pepper), the **long-distance** (the cloth, the pepper) — the **quantities** are to be fixed (the later ECO files).
