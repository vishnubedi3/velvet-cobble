> REL-02 fixes the **Veshna faith** in depth (resolves Q-013 [bookkeeping — the rite was fixed in DYN-02 §4 and is detailed here], Q-036, Q-038, Q-039, Q-040, Q-045, Q-069): the sacred corpus, the theology (the grammar), the temple network, the revivals, the de-consecrations, the other faiths, and the myths (the historical memory).

## 1. The sacred corpus (resolves Q-036)

The Veshna faith's corpus, in the **Veshna sacred script** (CUL-02):

1. **The Kesra Charter (the Kesra-Veshna)** — the **short foundational text**, attributed to the founding era (KE 0–35). The **dharma grammar** in its purest form: the dharma by station, the karma, the moksha, the king's duty as protector. It is **short** (a founding charter, not a long scripture) and is the **oldest text** (its orthography is frozen — CUL-02). **Q-078** (its full text) is intentionally unresolved (a narrative-stage artifact).
2. **The Veshna Shastras** — the **matha's dharma-shastra commentary cycle**, ~**60–80 works** across KE 40–500. The **legal + ritual interpretation** of the Charter (the dharma-shastra logic, REL-01). The **priestly class's self-reproduction** (the Shastras training, REL-01 §2).
3. **The Rites** — the **consecration rite** (fixed in DYN-02 §4; detailed here) + the **festival rites** (tied to the wind calendar, GEO-03).
4. **The Hagiography (the Kesra legend)** — the **founding myth** (the mythic Kesra I; §7). It is **later** than the Charter (the myth accretes over the eras — the High-Empire hagiography is the fullest).

## 2. The theology (the grammar, resolved for Q-036)

The Veshna faith is a **dharma grammar** (not a creed, REL-01):

- **The dharma by station (context-dependent):** the **king's dharma** = protection + justice; the **priest's dharma** = the rite; the **warrior's dharma** = defense; the **merchant's dharma** = honest trade (the **anti-usury ethic** the Tarnesh Dawn radicalizes, §5); the **peasant's dharma** = the cultivation + the tribute. Each duty is **context-dependent** (by varna, by stage, by time/place — the CUL-01 grammar).
- **The karma** — the **moral causation across lives** (the social glue; the **critique target** of the revivals — the "the karma justifies the jati" charge).
- **The moksha** — the **liberation** (the **priestly telos**; the **lay telos** = the dharma-fulfillment + the good death). The laity's salvation is **this-worldly** (the dharma done), the priest's is **other-worldly** (the moksha) — a **two-tier telos** (the lay devotion is the dharma-fulfillment, the priestly pursuit is the moksha).
- **The varna framework** — the **ideal frame** (contested from within by the revivals, §5); the **jati** is the **operative unit** (the DYN-01 §5 absorbed elite, the ADM-02 jati law).

## 3. The consecration rite (resolves Q-013; details DYN-02 §4)

- **The rite:** the **ritual investiture + the public oath before the Veshna temple**, performed by the **head of the Veshna matha (the guru)**. The king is **consecrated as the protector of dharma**. It is **public** (the court + the city witness — the legitimation is performative, DYN-01 §3).
- **The reversibility:** **reversible in principle** (the **de-consecration** — the matha acting with the house's consent) but **rarely used** (a last resort; de-legitimating a king is itself a legitimacy crisis). §6 fixes the canon count.
- **The Great Succession Crisis exception:** the **matha is split** (the orthodoxy wing consecrates the Threna I; the Tarnesh wing **withholds** its sanction — refusing to recognize that consecration, the "de-consecration in absentia," KE 525, TIM-03 §5) — the kingmaker is **divided** (the DYN-02 §4 "the matha split" is now **caused** — by the Tarnesh Dawn revival, §5).

## 4. The temple network (resolves Q-039)

The Veshna temple network (the tiered network, the Sanatan temple-economy logic, REL-01 §2):

- **1 Great Temple:** the **Veshna Great Temple** (at Veshna, the **guru's seat**, built in the Dharan I program, KE 240–265, TIM-03). The apex.
- **7 Regional Head Temples:** **Kesra, Besra, Voren, Threna, Keshkhor, Kesveth, Tarenam** (the Tarn head town — the 7th, the Tarn's religious glue, ADM-02). One per major region/city.
- **~250–400 Local Temples/Shrines:** the district/village shrines (the local devotion, the festival sites).
- **The land (the devanama, the tax-exempt parallel fiscal base):** the Great Temple ~**5%** of the Veshna region's arable; the **total temple land ~10–15%** of the arable (the REL-01 §2 "parallel landholding," the ADM-01 §7 "temple evasion" — the tax-exempt institutional property, the Islam waqf logic).
- **The administration:** the **matha (the apex)** appoints the **regional heads (the Veshna priests)**, collects the **offerings/tithes**, runs the **Shastras training** (the priestly class's self-reproduction). The **mobile node:** the matha's **branches (the "daughters")** can be **planted in a new region without the territory** (the Sanatan guru/matha logic, REL-01 §2) — the matha's power is **portable** (it outlives the territorial states, TIM-03's post-fragmentation).

## 5. The revivals (resolves Q-040)

The **orthodoxy-vs-revival** standing dynamic (REL-01 §4), fixed:

1. **The Khel Renewal (KE 250–320, the middle Oren):** the **vernacular devotion** (the Tarn/lowland drift, CUL-02); the **anti-varna-rigidity** stance (the "the dharma is not the jati" charge); the **leader: Khesh the Potter** (the name pool, DYN-02 §1 — a low-caste craftsman, the point). **The state:** tolerated → **patronized (KE 280)** (the court uses it to **check the matha's power** — the REL-01 §4 "a tool for the state"). **The orthodoxy:** condemned (KE 260) → partially absorbed (the Khel Renewal's vernacular is adopted into the festival rites — the syncretism).
2. **The Tarnesh Dawn (KE 405–460, the Tarn/delta):** the **famine/debt context** (the Stress, TIM-02); the **anti-usury dharma** (the "the honest trade vs. the moneylender" — the merchant's dharma radicalized, §2); the **leader: Tarnesh the Weaver** (the name pool). **The matha SPLIT over it** (the **Tarn wing patronizes** it; the **Veshna orthodoxy wing condemns** it) — **this split carries into the Great Succession Crisis** (the DYN-02 §4 "the matha split" is now **caused** — the Tarnesh wing's **withheld sanction**, KE 525, TIM-03 §5: the Tarn wing backs the Kesra III's claim and refuses to recognize the orthodoxy wing's consecration of the Threna I — the sanction is withheld **from the Threna I's consecration**, not from the Kesra III, the CC-02 fix).
3. **The local folk cults** (the standing, the un-fixed): the village shrine cults (the river shrines, the ancestors) — the **Tarn faith's base** (§6). They are **not** a single movement (they are the **substrate** the revivals draw on).

## 6. The other faiths (resolves Q-038)

The **religious pluralism** (REL-01 §5), fixed:

- **The Khoric faith (the steppe):** the **horse-cult + the ancestor-tally**; the **oral rite** (no temple — the **sacred horse + the tally-stones**); the **assembly blessing** (the Khor tally's oath, CUL-02). The **Khor's "priests"** are the **tally-keepers** (the assembly's recorders, FOR-01 §3).
- **The Veth faith (the Vethra):** the **Orenic drift** — a **Veshna variant** + the **sea-vessel rite + the trade blessing**; the **Veth temple network** (smaller than the Veshna — the Vethra is a "state too big," not a temple-empire); the **Vethra court patronizes it** (a **parallel** to the Veshna — the Vethra's legitimation, FOR-01 §2).
- **The Tarn faith (the Tarn plain):** the **local shrine cult** — the **river shrines + the ancestors + a Veshna-lite**; the **Tarenam head temple** is a **Veshna regional head temple** (the 7th, §4) **but** the **local shrines are Tarnic** — a **dual character** (the Veshna head temple over the Tarnic shrines — the hybrid, the ADM-02 Tarn glue).
- **The Phre faith (the Vorenavi):** the **maritime faith** — the **sea-vessel + the trade rite + the ship-blessing**; the **polytheistic maritime animism** (the sea-vessel, the trade-spirits); the **enclave temples** (the Voren enclaves, TIM-03). It is **not** an Orenic/Khoric cousin (a separate tradition, CUL-02).

## 7. The myths (the historical memory, resolves Q-045)

The **four memory layers** (the CUL-01 §5 "imperfect self-understanding," fixed):

1. **The Founding Myth (the Kesra legend):** the hero who **"drank the Oren"** and **"raised the Veshna mountain"** — an **exaggeration of the material founding** (the chokepoint + the temple, DYN-01 §1). **The distortion:** it **erases the chiefs' absorption as a conquest** (the myth makes the founding a **hero's deed**, not a **material takeover** — the DYN-01 §1 "material founding" is the hidden history).
2. **The Consecration Legend:** the **guru's blessing is the source of the king's power** — **the distortion:** it **erases the Shreshtha's role and the military** (the legend makes the king **sacred**, not **dual-structured** — the DYN-01 §3 "dual structure" is the hidden history).
3. **The Golden-Age Memory (the Varan I era = the "Kesra's fullness"):** **the distortion:** it **idealizes the peak and erases the Peshan I overstretch as "the wind's fault"** (the Golden Age is a **memory**, not a **fact** — the Peshan I's overstretch, TIM-02, is the hidden history).
4. **The Decline Narrative (the "abandoned by the wind" + "the Phre's betrayal"):** **the distortion:** it **externalizes the cause** (the wind, the Phre) and **erases the internal fiscal/elite causes** (the monetization chain, the dual-elite fragilization, TIM-01 root causes #3/#4 — the hidden history).

**The hidden-history layer:** **two source traditions disagree** — the **Veshna hagiography** (the mythic) vs. the **Beshara records** (the fiscal-administrative). **The gap between them is the hidden history** (Q-077, intentionally unresolved). The Beshara records (the tax rolls, the mint ledgers, the tribute tallies) are the **material record**; the Veshna hagiography is the **sacred record**; **they disagree on the founding, the overstretch, and the decline** (the four distortions above).

## 8. The de-consecrations (resolves Q-069)

The canon: **zero completed de-consecrations + one withheld sanction.**

- **The withheld sanction (the "de-consecration in absentia," KE 525):** the **Tarnesh wing's refusal to recognize the orthodoxy wing's consecration of Threna I** (the Great Succession Crisis, TIM-03 §5 — the Tarn wing backed the Kesra III's claim; the CC-02 fix) — a **partial de-legitimation** (the Tarn wing declares the rival consecration void), **not** a completed de-consecration. It is the **only** time the matha's sanction was **withheld** (a **deterrence**, not a **consummation**).
- **The explanatory value (the negative space, NS-01):** the matha's power **functions as a deterrence that is never consummated** — the de-consecration is **available** (it deters) but **never executed** (executing it would be a legitimacy crisis for the matha itself). And the **final collapse (KE 675, the House of Kesra's end, TIM-03) is political/economic** (the chiefs + the Phre's non-intervention), **not religious** — the matha **survives** precisely **because it did not consummate** (it did not tie its legitimacy to a house that failed). **The religious mechanism is bypassed in the final collapse** — an insight: the empire dies of **fiscal/elite** causes, not **religious** ones (the TIM-01 root-cause/trigger logic).
