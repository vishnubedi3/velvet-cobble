# Q-024 The specific legal codes (the four laws)

Status: RESOLVED → ADM-02 §2 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the specific legal codes (the king's law, the jati law, the temple law, the port law — the content, the judges, the enforcement)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the ADM-01 §5 fixed the four-law pluralism but not the named codes/judges/jurisdiction.
- Evidence/reasoning: the Sanatan dharma-shastra legal pluralism + the Islam qadi/madhhab (the judges) + the CUL-02 §7 functional language map.
- Alternatives considered: a single code (rejected — the ADM-01 §5 pluralism); a court hierarchy only (rejected — the parallel laws are the ADM-01 §5).
- Selected resolution: the **4 laws** — the king's law = the Beshara Code (the revenue law: the land tax, the monopolies, the contracts, the weights/measures) + the Dhresh Code (the military law: the conscription, the garrison, the desertion), the royal judges (the Beshara judges, the Dhresh judges); the jati law (the panchayat: the marriage, the guild discipline, the purity, the minor civil, the panchayat elders); the temple law (the rite, the consecration, the endowments, the orthodoxy disputes, the Veshna priests, the apex = the matha); the port law (the charter, the freight, the customs, the maritime, the Vorenesh + the port merchants, the pidgin/sea-script, from KE 500 the Phre enclave law = the extraterritoriality). The jurisdiction/appeals: the jati → the royal judge → the court (the Shreshtha, the apex); the temple law is parallel; the port law is parallel; the jurisdictional conflicts → the Shreshtha decides.
- Why chosen: it is the ADM-01 §5 four-law pluralism made nominally/judicially/jurisdictionally operational, with the CUL-02 §7 language map.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the ADM-02 §2, the CUL-02 §7 (the language map), the REL-02 (the temple law).
- Canon status: CANON (ADM-02 §2).
