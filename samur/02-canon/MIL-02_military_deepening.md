## 1. The army sizes (resolves Q-031)

The **High-Empire peak** (KE ~300, the ~35M population, the DEM-02 §1):

| Unit | Strength (the High-Empire peak) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **The core field army** | **40–50k** | the infantry 25–30k, the cavalry 8–12k, the artillery/siege 2–3k, the elephants 200–400, the seasonal irregulars/gupta 10–15k (not standing) |
| **The Sareth garrison** | **8–12k** | the Threna 4–6k, the Gheshar 2–3k, the Ghul 1–2k (the ADM-02 §1 frontier generals) |
| **The Khor garrison** | **6–10k** | the Keshkhor + the mobile cavalry (the MIL-01 §6 hard frontier) |
| **The delta/coast** | **5–8k** | the Voren garrison + the marines (the MIL-01 §6) |
| **The Tarn levy** | **10–15k** | the houses' quota (the ADM-02 §3, the DYN-03 houses) |

- **The standing total:** ~**35–45k**; the **full mobilization:** **80–110k** (a **~0.3% standing rate** of the ~35M — the **recruit pool ceiling**, the DEM-02 §3 "the army can never exceed a ~0.3% standing rate without breaking the labor supply").
- **The Stress (KE 380–500):** ~**50–70k** mobilization (the **fiscal strain**, the TIM-02 — the army contracts as the revenue fails).
- **The Fragmentation (KE 500–680):** the **dispersal** (the successor states field their own — the TIM-03).

## 2. The cavalry (resolves Q-034)

- **The Khor horse trade quota:** **2–4k horses/year** (the High Empire; via **Keshkhor** — the replacement rate, the ~1/3 of the 8–12k cavalry's annual attrition — the consistent).
- **The price:** the **bār + the silver + the textile per horse** (the ECO-02 §6 trade).
- **The quality:** the **steppe stock** (the **hardy, fast, small** — the **light-cavalry horse**; the **heavy** is a **minority elite import** — the court's war-mounts, the DYN-02 military houses).
- **The composition:** the **shiledar** (the mounted warriors of the **martial castes**, the DYN-01 §5) + the **Khor-mounted Samur** + the **Khor contingents** (the **allied/mercenary horse-people** — the Khor clans fight for the Samur in the campaigns, the DYN-03 §Khor clans).
- **The dependency (the strategic vulnerability, the MIL-01 §4):** a **Khor bad-grass year** (the GEO-03 §3 ~7–10 years) / the **Khor crisis** (the Zhan Ur raid, KE 371, TIM-03) → the **horse shortfall** → the **cavalry shortfall** → the **strategic vulnerability**. **The Keshkhor** = the **buffer + the trade + the early-warning** (the MIL-01 §6).

## 3. The navy/marines (resolves Q-035)

- **The Oren river fleet:** the **barges** (the merchant, the state-leased — the ECO-02 §7 "hundreds of barges") + the **armed river-craft** (the patrol/escort — the **few dozen**).
- **The delta coastal fleet:** the **Voren dockyards** — the **coastal traders + the armed merchantmen + the few war-crafts** (the **~a dozen war-crafts at the peak**); the **marines ~2–5k** (the Voren garrison, §1).
- **The limit (the negative space, the NS-01):** the Samur navy is a **coast-guard + a river fleet, NOT a blue-water navy** — **why no blue-water fleet?** the **cost + the Phre's superiority + the Samur's strategy = the delta-mouth control, not the open sea** (the **integration cost** — the NS-01 logic: the open sea is not worth the fleet, the delta mouth is). **The Phre (KE 500) → the outmatched** (the **sea-lane control** passes to the Phre — the TIM-03, the MIL-01 §6).

## 4. The artillery + metallurgy (resolves Q-033; consolidates Q-053, Q-064; resolves Q-052)

- **The state workshops (the karkhanas):** the **Kesra, the Threna, the Voren** (the **Dharan I program** founds the Threna/Voren, KE 250–270, TIM-03; the **Varan I** expands — the ECO-02 §8).
- **The types:** the **barrage gun** (the **bronze/iron cannon** — the **large stationary, the small mobile**; the siege) + the **Voren rocket** (the **reed-tube + the charcoal-powder** — the **harassment/signal**; the Mysore logic).
- **The metallurgy (resolves Q-052):** the **Sareth/southern iron** (the GEO-01 §5); the **bronze** (the **copper from the Sareth foothills + the tin via the Khor trade** — the Khor's mineral export, the FOR-01 §3); the **steel** (the **high-value** — the **court demand + the military**, the ECO-02 §8 dual demand).
- **The deployment:** the **siege** (the Vethra fort — the MIL-01 §6), the **pass defense** (the Sareth — the ADM-02 §1), the **delta defense** (the Voren — the MIL-01 §6).
- **The scale:** the **High-Empire peak:** the **few hundred guns, the few thousand rockets/year** (the workshops' output, the ECO-02 §8).
- **The operators:** the **Dhresh gunners** (the **specialist corps, 2–3k** — the MIL-01 §3).

## Consequences (immediate / unintended / long-term)

- **Immediate:** the army sizes (the 40–50k field, the garrisons, the 80–110k mobilization, the 0.3% ceiling), the cavalry (the 2–4k/year quota, the steppe stock, the shiledar/Khor composition, the dependency), the navy/marines (the river fleet, the ~a dozen war-crafts, the 2–5k marines, the no-blue-water negative space), the artillery/metallurgy (the karkhanas, the barrage gun/Voren rocket, the bronze/steel, the few hundred guns), the craft guilds (the silk/steel/textile/paper/dye, the guild law), the river/sea logistics (the barge, the dockyard, the supply line, the northward failure mode), the war elephant (the 200–400, the mahout, the specialty).
- **Unintended:** the **0.3% standing-rate ceiling** is a **structural limit** (the army **cannot** exceed it without breaking the labor supply — the DEM-02 §3; the **overstretch is not a choice, it is a ceiling** — the MIL-01 §8); the **no-blue-water-navy** is a **strategic choice** (the delta-mouth control, the NS-01) — it **succeeds** (the Samur holds the delta mouth) **until the Phre arrives** (the KE 500, the TIM-03 — the Phre does **not** need the blue water, it **owns** the sea-lanes); the **Voren rocket** is the **military's answer to the Phre's sea-lane control** (the **asymmetric** — the rocket is the Samur's **range** answer to the Phre's **sea** answer — a TIM-03 detail).
- **Long-term:** the **0.3% ceiling + the three-front condition** is the **military root cause** of the overstretch (the TIM-01 root cause #1, made operational — the army **cannot** hold three fronts at the 0.3% ceiling without the **irregulars (the gupta)** — the MIL-01 §3 "cannot be stood down" is the **overstretch's military expression**); the **successor states inherit the military template** (the Tarn Confederation's levy, the Sareth Dhaneshra's garrison, the Kesra Dhaneshra's field army — the TIM-03); the **Voren rocket** is the **post-fragmentation military seed** (the Q-079).
