# Q-003 The political structure of the Tarn plain

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01 (§4, §8.3); will constrain ADM-01 (tributary administration), the Phase 5 fragmentation site
Why it matters: the Tarn plain is the tributary periphery and the future fragmentation site; its internal structure (one tributary polity vs. several local elites) determines how the empire extracts revenue from it and what the successor states look like when the empire weakens.
Current best guesses: several local elites under a nominal tributary arrangement (the "inner colony" pattern) rather than one consolidated tributary polity — consolidation there would be a *later* development to be causally tracked.
