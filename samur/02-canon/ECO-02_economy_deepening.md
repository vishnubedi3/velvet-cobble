## 1. The tax rates (resolves Q-022)

The **High-Empire standard rates** (the Beshara Code, ADM-02 §2):

| Tax | Rate | Form |
|---|---|---|
| **The bār land tax (the "quarter duty")** | **1/4 of the assessed yield** | in kind (the bār) → **in silver** (the monetization: assessed in silver, paid in the grain at the market price — the **peasant must sell**, the GEO-01 §8.4 monetization chain) |
| **The wartime levy (the "Dhresh duty")** | **+1/8 in the war years** | the **Stress-era inflation mechanism** (the Peshan I reform, KE 350, TIM-02 — the overstretch's fiscal arm) |
| **The pell tax** | **1/5 of the yield** | the **pell is more productive** (the lower rate, the larger volume) + the **port dues** |
| **The transit/market dues** | **2–5%** | at the passes, the ports, the major markets (the Beshara collectors, ADM-02) |
| **The Tarn tribute (the Tarnesh fourth)** | **1/4 of the net pell harvest** + the crossings' tolls | the **1/2 remitted** to the Samur treasury (the ADM-02 §3) |
| **The temple** | **exempt** | the **devanama** (the REL-02 §4 10–15% temple land) |
| **The monopolies** | the **state price** | the salt, the horse, the pepper (§2) |

**The inflation mechanism (the Stress, TIM-02):** the **Dhresh duty (the +1/8)** + the **debasement (the mint, §3)** + the **assessment inflation (the Beshara raises the assessed yield)** = the **revenue targets inflate** → the **peasant must sell more** → the **moneylender debt** → the **revolt** (the GEO-01 §8.4 monetization chain, the TIM-02 "first major peasant revolt KE 400–408").

## 5. The Veth pepper (resolves Q-005)

The **Vetham (the Veth pepper)** — the **high-value aromatic**:

- **The botany:** a **climbing shrub** of the **Veth hill-slopes**; needs the **monsoon (the sea-wind, GEO-03) + the humid slope**; the **small plots, the high value**.
- **The cure:** picked **green**, **sun-dried/fermented** (the **labor-intensive cure** — the Vethra's labor base, the FOR-01 §2).
- **The trade form:** the **dried pod**; the **"pepper-basket"** unit (the trade unit).
- **The economic logic:** the **high value-to-weight** → the **maritime trade** (the Voren/Kesveth, the ECO-01 §3); the **monopoly logic** (the **small area, the high value, the easy state control** at Kesveth/Voren — §2).
- **The wind dependence (the GEO-03 §6 link):** a **failed wind → the pepper fails → the price spikes** (the **Vethra's fiscal shock** — the Vethra is also wind-dependent, the GEO-03 §6 shared vulnerability).
