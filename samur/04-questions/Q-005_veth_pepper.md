# Q-005 The nature of the Veth pepper / coastal goods

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01 (§5, §8.5); will constrain ECO-01 (trade structure), FOR (Vethra's economy), the Phre chartered company's commodity anchor
Why it matters: the coastal trade goods are the reason the delta and the Veth shore matter at all; whether "the pepper" is one dominant cash crop or a range of goods shapes Vethra's craft/irrigation economy, the empire's southern rivalry, and what the chartered company trades for.
Current best guesses: one dominant high-value aromatic (the "Veth pepper") plus a range of secondary goods (fine textiles, salt, timber) — the single-crop anchor makes Vethra's strategic value sharper and its economy more vulnerable.
