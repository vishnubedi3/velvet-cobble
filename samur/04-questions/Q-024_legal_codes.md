# Q-024 Specific legal codes (the four laws)

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ADM-01 (§5); constrains REL-01 (the temple law), CUL-01 (the legal pluralism)
Why it matters: the specific content, judges, and enforcement of the four laws (the king's law, the jati law, the temple law, the port law) define the legal order and its fault lines — the *overlap* between the laws (the jurisdictional competition) is a source of tension (the state vs. the caste/temple/port).
Current best guesses: the **king's law** (the public order, the revenue, the military — the state's judges); the **jati law** (the internal caste law — the panchayat's elders); the **temple law** (the ritual/moral law — the Veshna matha's priests); the **port law** (the maritime/trade law — the port's merchants/officials) — the **king's law is overarching** (the public order), the other three are **internal/specialized** (the bounded pluralism, the madhhab logic).
