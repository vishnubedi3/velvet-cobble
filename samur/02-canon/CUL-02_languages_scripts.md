# CUL-02 The Languages and Scripts of Veshran

Status: CANON
Date: 2026-08-26
Last revised: 2026-08-26
Depends on: CUL-01, DYN-02 (the name pool), ADM-01/02 (the legal codes' languages), FOR-01/02 (the foreign powers), REL-01 (the faiths)
Dependents: ADM-02 (the legal codes), REL-02 (the revival's vernaculars), TIM-03 (the Phre documents), the narrative phase (if authorized — the dialogue registers)
Sources: CUL-01 §2 (the multi-lingual empire, the coastal trade pidgin), comparative: `mughal.md` (the Persian/Arabic/vernacular layering), `qing.md` (the Manchu/Chinese dual script), `judaism.md` (the portable written law)
Influence: CUL-02 (see the register)

> CUL-02 fixes the **language map and the scripts** (resolves Q-042, Q-043, Q-044; consolidates the duplicate Q-074, Q-075). It extends the CUL-01 toponymic register to the **foreign languages** and defines the **scripts**. The Samur-language register itself is unchanged (CUL-01, fixed).

## 1. The language families

- **The Orenic family** (named for the Oren): the **Samur language** (the core) + the **Veth language** (the coastal drift) + the **Tarn lowland varieties** (the plain drift). One family, three registers.
- **The Khoric family** (the steppe): the **Khor language** — a separate family (the steppe isolation; not an Orenic cousin).
- **The Phreic family** (the island): the **Phre language** — a separate, distant family (the island maritime).
- **The delta pidgin**: a **contact language** — an Orenic base + Phreic/Veth trade vocabulary (the CUL-01 §2 "coastal trade pidgin," now fixed). The lingua franca of the Voren.

## 2. The Samur language (fixed in CUL-01; the script now fixed)

- **The register:** the consonant-rich register (CUL-01) — the -a/-an/-esh/-ath suffixes, the k/kh/g/gh/t/th/p/b/ṣ/s/sh/r/l/m/n/v/y/h/z + a/ā/i/ī/u/ū/e/o.
- **The script: the Orenic script** — an **abugida syllabary** (~**40 characters**), carved/inked. The language of the king's law, the court, the Beshara/Dhresh codes (ADM-02).
- **The Veshna sacred script** — an **older** abugida (~**60 characters**, with the **sacred diacritics** for the ritual sounds). The language of the Kesra Charter, the Veshna Shastras, the consecration rite (REL-02). It is **conservative** (it changes slower than the Orenic script — the sacred text's orthography is frozen).

## 3. The Khor language (resolves the Khor component of Q-042/Q-075)

- **The family:** **Khoric** (separate from Orenic — the steppe isolation).
- **The phonology:** a **richer vowel system** (~**6 vowels**, including the back vowels the Samur lacks); **no th/gh fricative clusters** (the Samur's signature clusters are absent); the **agglutinative** suffixes (-**ur** for rank/office, -**an** for kinship).
- **The script: none.** The Khor is **oral** — the **knot-tally (the Khor tally)** for the assembly records (the knotted cord, the steppe's "writing"); the **horse-tally marks** for the horse trade (the brands/tally at Keshkhor). The Khor's "documents" are the **tally** + the **oral oath** (the assembly's word is binding, FOR-01 §3).

## 4. The Tarn languages (resolves Q-042)

- **Not one language but 2–3 Orenic lowland varieties** (the Tarn is a plain of several houses, ADM-02): the **Keshath variety** (the north), the **Taren variety** (the central), the **Orenam variety** (the south/delta fringe).
- **The drift:** a **softer** Orenic drift (more vowels, fewer clusters, the -**an/ -am** suffixes) + a **local substrate** (the plain's pre-Samur vocabulary).
- **The trade register:** the **Samur-Tarn trade register** (a simplified Orenic + the Tarn varieties) — the **tribute administration language** (the Tarn governor, the tribute tallies, ADM-02).

## 5. The Veth language (resolves Q-043; consolidates Q-074)

- **The family:** **Orenic coastal drift** (a Vethra cousin of the Samur).
- **The drift:** the **maritime loanwords** (from the Phreic/trade — the sea-vocabulary), the **lighter consonant clusters** (the coastal speech), the -**esh/-eth** suffixes.
- **The script:** an **adapted Orenic syllabary** + the **trade characters** (the added characters for the Phreic/trade sounds the Orenic script lacks).
- **The Veth faith's liturgy** is in the Veth language (the Veshna variant + the sea-vessel rite — REL-02 §other-faiths).

## 6. The Phre language (resolves Q-044; consolidates the Phre component of Q-075)

- **The family:** **Phreic** (separate, distant — the island).
- **The phonology:** a **different consonant inventory** (no aspirates — the Phreic lacks the Samur's kh/gh/th/dh aspirates), the -**an/-or** suffixes.
- **The script: the sea-script** — a **logographic trade script** (the enclaves' documents, the ship's papers, the Voren enclave law). It is **not** an Orenic/Khoric cousin (a separate writing tradition).
- **The delta pidgin** (§1) is the **interlanguage** between the Phreic and the Orenic (the Voren's working language).

## 7. The functional language map (who speaks/writes what)

| Domain | Language | Script |
|---|---|---|
| The king's law (the Beshara/Dhresh codes) | the Samur | the Orenic script |
| The temple law (the rite, the consecration, the Shastras) | the Samur (the sacred register) | the Veshna sacred script |
| The jati law | the Samur (the jati variety) + the oral | the jati marks (the panchayat's tallies) |
| The port law | the delta pidgin + the Phreic | the sea-script + the Orenic |
| The Phre documents (the enclaves, the ships) | the Phreic | the sea-script (the Samur officials read them via the **Voren interpreters** — a dependency, TIM-03) |
| The Tarn tribute administration | the Samur-Tarn trade register | the Orenic script (the tallies) |
| The Khor trade/assembly | the Khoric | the knot-tally (oral + the tally) |
| The revival literature (the vernacular) | the Tarn/Veth vernaculars | the Orenic script (the vernacular in the core script) |
| The foreign courts (the Vethra, the Khor, the Phre) | the Veth/Khoric/Phreic | the Veth script / the tally / the sea-script |

## Consequences (immediate / unintended / long-term)

- **Immediate:** the language families (the Orenic/Khoric/Phreic + the pidgin), the scripts (the Orenic, the Veshna sacred, the sea-script, the knot-tally), the functional map (who speaks/writes what), the foreign registers (the Khor/Tarn/Veth/Phre).
- **Unintended:** the **Phre documents are in the sea-script** — the Samur **depends on the Voren interpreters** to read the Phre (a **knowledge dependency** — the Phre controls its own documents, a TIM-03 friction point); the **Veshna sacred script is frozen** (the sacred orthography is conservative — the matha controls the "correct" sacred writing, a REL-02 authority source); the **revival literature in the vernacular** (the Tarn/Veth drifts in the core script) is the **democratization of the written word** (the CUL-01 §3 literary culture, the REL-02 §revival).
- **Long-term:** the **language map is the post-fragmentation cultural map** (the successor states speak the Tarn/Sareth/Khor varieties; the Vethra speaks the Veth; the Phre enclaves speak the Phreic/pidgin — the **cultural boundaries** of the post-fragmentation, Q-079).
