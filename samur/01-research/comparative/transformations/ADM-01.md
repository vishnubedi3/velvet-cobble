# Transformation Log — ADM-01 (Administration and Government)

Date: 2026-08-26
Canon target: **ADM-01** (the court at Kesra, the dual elite, the tiered provincial government — direct/garrison/tribute/port, the revenue/zabt/state monopoly/monetization chain, the silver standard/dual currency, the four-law pluralism, the infrastructure, the tax evasion)
Method: the 5-step sequential reasoning.

## Step 1 — Historical examples and underlying mechanisms

- **Mughal** — the **zabt** (land revenue assessment), the **salaried mansab office**, the **court as the state**, the **cash-revenue state**, the **state monopoly** (the salt, the horses, the textiles), the **transit/market dues**.
- **Vijayanagara** — the **layered jurisdiction** (the amranayaka/dandanayaka/village), the **professional clerk class** (the Vellala), the **chief system**, the **temple economy** (the fiscal sink, the parallel landholding).
- **Maratha** — the **chauth/sardeshmukhi** (tribute without administration), the **shahukar** (the merchant banker), the **Peshwa's bureaucracy**.
- **Qing** — the **tiered sovereignty** (the direct core, the frontier), the **frontier generals** (the fuheng), the **co-opted bureaucracy**, the **overseers** (the dou).
- **Sanatan** — the **dharma-shastra legal pluralism** (the king's law + the corporate law + the temple law), the **village panchayat**, the **temple economy**.
- **Islam** — the **qadi** (the judge), the **waqf** (the tax-exempt institutional property), the **madhhab** (the bounded legal pluralism).

## Step 2 — Original historical conditions

- The Mughal zabt + the court + the state monopoly.
- The Vijayanagara layered jurisdiction + the clerk class + the temple.
- The Maratha chauth + the shahukar.
- The Qing tiered sovereignty + the frontier generals + the co-opted bureaucracy.
- The Sanatan legal pluralism + the panchayat.
- The Islam qadi + the waqf + the madhhab.

## Step 3 — Equivalent or contrasting Samur conditions

- The Samur central government is the **court at Kesra** (the Shreshtha + the king + the temple + the chiefs + the merchant/bankers) — the **dual elite** (the founding elite holds the top offices, the absorbed elite holds the lower/revenue offices).
- The Samur provincial government is **tiered** (the direct bār core, the garrisoned borders, the tributary east, the port coast) — the **tiered sovereignty** by geography/administrative cost (not ethnicity).
- The Samur revenue is the **zabt + the state monopoly + the monetization chain** (the silver cash → the debt → the revolt).
- The Samur currency is the **silver standard** (the dual currency: the silver coin + the grain) — the **monetization is tied to the frontier** (the silver from the Khor/Sareth).
- The Samur legal order is the **four-law pluralism** (the king's law + the jati law + the temple law + the port law) — the **dharma-shastra logic** + the **madhhab logic** (the bounded pluralism).

**Contrasts (the load-bearing differences):** the **tiered control is by geography/administrative cost** (not ethnicity, unlike the Qing); the **monetization is tied to the frontier** (the silver from the Khor/Sareth — a failed frontier is a monetary crisis, unlike the Mughal where the silver is more internal); the **four-law pluralism** (the king's + the jati's + the temple's + the port's — the **port law** is a Samur-specific addition, given the maritime/delta emphasis).

## Step 4 — Original Samur solution

The **Samur administration** — the court at Kesra (the Shreshtha + the king + the temple + the chiefs + the merchant/bankers, the dual elite); the tiered provincial government (the direct bār core, the garrisoned borders, the tributary east, the port coast); the revenue (the zabt + the state monopoly + the monetization chain); the currency (the silver standard, the dual currency); the legal pluralism (the four laws); the infrastructure (the Oren, the irrigation, the roads, the garrisons, the port) — is the **Samur-specific answer** to the **Samur conditions** (the Oren spine, the Sareth wall, the Khor steppe, the Veth coast, the Tarn plain; the five factions; the dual elite; the frontier silver). It is **not a reskin**: the **tiered control by geography** (not ethnicity), the **monetization tied to the frontier** (a failed frontier is a monetary crisis), and the **four-law pluralism** (the port law) are the load-bearing differences.

## Step 5 — Consequences

- **Immediate:** the central government, the tiered provincial government, the revenue/monetization, the currency, the legal pluralism, the infrastructure.
- **Unintended:** the tax evasion is structural (the five resisters) — the revenue is always contested (the fiscal-military tension); the monetization ties the fiscal engine to the frontier (a failed frontier is a monetary crisis); the tiered control makes the empire uneven (the fragmentation site).
- **Long-term:** the revenue contest is a root cause of the fiscal stress (TIM-01); the monetization chain is the social fault line (DEM-01); the tiered control is the fragmentation site (the tributary east → the successor states); the dual elite is the elite fault line (DYN-01).
