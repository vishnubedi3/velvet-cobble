# Q-038 The specific Khor/Veth/Tarn/port faiths

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: REL-01 (§5); constrains FOR-01 (the Khor/Veth/Phre), the religious pluralism
Why it matters: the specific Khor/Veth/Tarn/port faiths (the theologies, the institutions, the relationship to the Veshna faith) define the religious pluralism and the post-imperial religious map — the Vethra's faith (the "state too strong") and the Phre's port faith (the Fragmentation) are the two most consequential.
Current best guesses: the **Khor faith** (the steppe religion — the ancestral/shamanic, the mobile), the **Veth faith** (the Vethra's religion — the compact state's temple/faith, the rivalry), the **Tarn faiths** (the tributary east's local religions — the plural, the tributary), the **port faith** (the Phre's religion — the maritime, the chartered company) — the **specific theologies** and the **relationship to the Veshna faith** are to be fixed (FOR-01).
