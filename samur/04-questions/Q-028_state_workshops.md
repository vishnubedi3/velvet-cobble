- Evidence/reasoning: the Mysore state workshops (the karkhanas, the silk/steel) + the ECO-01 §6 "the dual demand" + the Dharan I program (the TIM-03 §4).

- Why chosen: it is the Mysore state workshops + the ECO-01 §6 dual demand made nominally/locationally operationally operational.
