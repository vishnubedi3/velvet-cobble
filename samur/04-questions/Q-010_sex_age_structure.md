# Q-010 Sex/age structure

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DEM-01; will constrain MIL-01 (the recruitment), the demographic cycle
Why it matters: the **recruit pool** (the working-age males) and the **dependent ratio** (the old, the very young) determine the army's size and the tax base. A frontier empire with a standing army draws heavily on the working-age males.
Current best guesses: a **broad working-age cohort** (the agrarian surplus) with a **high dependent ratio** (the pre-industrial mortality) — the **recruit pool** is the **working-age males of the dense agrarian core** (a small % of the total population, but a large absolute number given the ~25–40 million).
