### CC-08 — The long-reign cluster: individually characterized rulers with reigns beyond the canon's human lifespan (priority 8)

- **Conflict:** `DEM-02 §3` fixes the human scale (life expectancy ~35–40 at birth, ~55–60 reaching adulthood). Yet several individually characterized rulers carry reign spans no human can reach: **Threna I (KE 525–650 = 125 years)** — consecrated KE 525 (`TIM-03 §5`), married ~KE 560 (`DYN-03 §2 #4`), "the Threna I's long reign" with reforms KE 570/580 and deposition KE 650 (`TIM-03 §6`; `DYN-02 §2`), in a dynastic list whose other reigns run 25–65 years; **Vethan II (~KE 550–680 = 130 years)** (`FOR-02 §2`; `TIM-03 §8`; `DYN-04 §17` "Vethan II's reign ends at KE 680"); **Khesh Ur II (~KE 550–680 = 130 years)** (`FOR-02 §3`; `TIM-03 §8`) — an *elected* assembly leader. FOR-02's ranges are systematically era-length (Ghoran Ur ~180–300, Zhan Ur ~300–450, Kesveth I ~300–420, Tarnesh I ~420–550).
- **Why this cycle leaves it unresolved:** every resolution path exceeds this cycle's mandate without manufacturing canon: (a) shortening a reign requires a successor for the vacated span — a new named ruler, which the canon reserves as narrative detail (Q-081) and the charter's no-filler rule forbids manufacturing; (b) reinterpreting the spans as house/regnal-era periods is a structural revision of DYN-02/FOR-02/TIM-03 §8/DYN-04 §17/WORLD-MODEL requiring a design decision (era-naming conventions) the canon does not yet warrant. Per the stewardship principle ("if a matter is not ready for resolution, leave it unresolved — no provisional canon"), CC-08 is **preserved for a later cycle** with its two candidate resolution paths recorded.
- **Status:** OPEN — PRESERVED for a later cycle.
