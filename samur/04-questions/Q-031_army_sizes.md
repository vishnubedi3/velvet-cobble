# Q-031 Specific army sizes

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: MIL-01 (§2, §3); constrains the later MIL files, the wars (TIM-02+)
Why it matters: the specific army sizes (the infantry, the cavalry, the elephants, the artillery, the irregulars, the marines — the numbers, by era) define the military's scale and the fiscal-military tension (the DEM-01 geometry) — the garrison is small (the border is thin) and the core field army is the mobile strike force.
Current best guesses: a **core field army** of ~50–100k (the cavalry + the infantry + the artillery + the elephants) in the High Empire; the **garrisons** (the Sareth, the Khor) of ~10–30k each; the **chiefs' levies** (the Tarn) of ~20–50k (the district force); the **irregulars** (the gupta/dash) of ~10–30k (the raiding force) — the **numbers** are to be fixed by era (the later MIL files).
