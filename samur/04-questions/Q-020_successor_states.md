# Q-020 The specific successor states

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-01 (§3, §5), DYN-01 (§5, §7), GEO-02 (§1); constrains the Fragmentation / the composite-monarchy files, FOR-01
Why it matters: the successor states (the chiefs' districts → states) are the *post-imperial* phase — their specific form (which districts, their names, their centers, their dates) defines the post-Samur political map and the Phre's vassals.
Current best guesses: **three main successor states** mirroring the three frontiers + the core:
- an **eastern state** from the **Tarn Marches** (the richest, the tributary plain — the "Bengal" analog),
- a **western state** from the **Sareth Marches** (the pass fortress, the iron/timber — the "Awadh" analog),
- a **core/northern state** from the **bār core + the Khor Frontier** (the heartland, the horse trade — the "Peshwa" analog),
plus **Vethra** (the "state too strong" that *survives* and rises) and the **Phre's delta enclave** (the reverse-invasion axis). The **temple (the Veshna matha)** survives as a **religious institution** across all of them (the mobile node).
