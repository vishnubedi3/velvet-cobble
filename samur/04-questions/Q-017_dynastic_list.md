# Q-017 The dynastic list of the House of Kesra

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-01 (§3), DYN-01 (§4, §5); constrains DYN-02 (the kings), the succession crises
Why it matters: the individual kings (their KE dates, names, and the character of each reign) are the spine of the timeline and the succession crises — a weak king at the wrong moment (the Stress era) is where the Shreshtha's dominance and the Great Succession Crisis become possible.
Current best guesses: ~12–18 kings over the ~650 years of the House (an average reign of ~35–55 years, a plausible pre-industrial range); a **strong founder** (Kesra I), a **consolidator or two** (the Expansion), a **peak emperor** (the High Empire), then a **string of weaker kings** (the Stress) — the weakening of the crown over time is the precondition for the Shreshtha's dominance and the Great Succession Crisis.
