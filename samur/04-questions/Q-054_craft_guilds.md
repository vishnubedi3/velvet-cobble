Status: OPEN

Current best guesses: the **craft guilds** (the silk, the steel, the textiles, the paper, ECO-01 §6) — the **urban producers** (Besra, Voren, Kesra) — the **court demand** (the market anchor, the display) + the **trade** (the export, the textiles) — the **specific guilds** are to be fixed (the later TEC/ECO files).
