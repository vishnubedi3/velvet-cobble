# Q-012 The founding Shreshtha

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-02 §3 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-01 (§3), TIM-01; DYN-02 (§3); the King vs. Shreshtha fault line
Why it mattered: the first holder of the prime-ministerial/regent office sets the precedent for the office's power and its relationship to the crown.
**Resolution (now canon, DYN-02 §3):** the founding Shreshtha is **Gheshar I** (KE 0–40) — a senior general of the Samur warrior band, a fellow founding-family man, **not of the royal house**. This keeps the office *outside* the royal house (so the dual structure is real) while making it *founding* (not a later appointment).
