> ADM-02 fixes the **administration in depth** (resolves Q-021, Q-024, Q-003 [consolidating the duplicate Q-008], Q-063): the central offices, the legal codes, and the Tarn tribute governance.

## 1. The central offices (resolves Q-021)

The **High-Empire standard court** (from the Dharan I program, KE ~230 onward; TIM-03). The offices are **salaried, non-hereditary** (the DYN-01 §3 mansab logic — the stipend + the estate); the **dual elite** division of labor (the DYN-01 §5: the **military offices = the founding elite**, the **revenue/clerk offices = the absorbed elite**):

| Office | Holder (the dual elite) | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **The Shreshtha** | the founding elite (the military houses — the Gheshar house dominates, DYN-03) | the prime minister/regent; the army + the revenue; the head of the bureaucracy |
| **The Beshara** | the absorbed elite (the clerk class) | the revenue head; the land tax, the monetization, the mint oversight, the Beshara Code |
| **The Dhresh** | the founding elite (the military houses) | the military head; the army, the garrisons, the campaigns, the Dhresh Code |
| **The Guru** | the Veshna matha (a religious office, not a court stipend) | the matha head; the consecration, the temple law, the Shastras (REL-02) |
| **The Kesrath** | the founding elite (the military houses) | the court steward; the household, the patronage, the market-anchor procurement (the court as engine, ECO-01 §6) |
| **The Vorenesh** | the absorbed elite (the clerk/merchant class) | the port head (est. KE 155 with the Voren); the port law, the transit dues, the fleet; **from KE 500 a dual authority with the Phre governor** (a TIM-03 friction point) |
| **The frontier generals** (the Sareth Dhresh, the Khor Dhresh) | the founding elite (the military houses) | the garrison commanders, **wide autonomy at the frontier** (the Qing fuheng logic, ADM-01 §2) |

**The hierarchy:** the Shreshtha > the Beshara ≈ the Dhresh > the Vorenesh ≈ the Kesrath > the frontier generals > the clerks. **The appointment:** the **king appoints** (on the **Shreshtha's recommendation** — the Shreshtha's power, the DYN-01 §3 fault line). **The clerk class:** the **Beshara's assessors + the Vorenesh's port clerks + the Tarn governor's scribes** (the **absorbed elite's professional class**, the Vijayanagara Vellala logic — the ADM-01 §2 "clerks collect").

## 2. The legal codes (resolves Q-024)

The **four-law pluralism** (ADM-01 §5), fixed:

1. **The king's law = the Beshara Code + the Dhresh Code** (the Orenic script, CUL-02):
   - **The Beshara Code** (the revenue law): the land tax, the monopolies, the contracts, the weights/measures.
   - **The Dhresh Code** (the military law): the conscription, the garrison, the desertion.
   - **The judges:** the **royal judges** (the Beshara judges, the Dhresh judges), appointed by the Beshara/Dhresh.
2. **The jati law** (the panchayat, the oral + the jati marks): the marriage, the guild discipline, the purity rules, the minor civil disputes. **The judges:** the **panchayat elders** (the ADM-01 §5).
3. **The temple law** (the Veshna sacred script, CUL-02): the rite, the consecration, the endowments, the orthodoxy disputes. **The judges:** the **Veshna priests**; the **apex = the matha** (REL-02).
4. **The port law** (the delta pidgin + the sea-script, CUL-02): the charter, the freight, the customs, the maritime disputes. **The judges:** the **Vorenesh + the port merchants**; **from KE 500 the Phre's enclave law** (the **extraterritoriality** — the Phre documents in the sea-script, the Voren interpreters, CUL-02 §7).

**The jurisdiction/appeals:** the **jati** (the internal) → the **royal judge** (the public order/revenue) → the **court (the Shreshtha, the apex)**; the **temple law** is **parallel** (the ritual — it does not go to the royal court); the **port law** is **parallel** (the maritime). The **jurisdictional conflicts** (e.g., a trade contract that is also a jati dispute) → the **Shreshtha decides** (a **source of the court's power** — the ADM-01 §1 "the state is the chief instrument of power").

## 3. The Tarn tribute governance (resolves Q-003; consolidates Q-008; resolves Q-063)

The **Tarn Marches** are a **tribute confederation** (the ADM-01 §2 "held by tribute," the Maratha chauth logic — the tribute without administration):

- **The 3 houses** (the DYN-03 names): the **Keshath** (the north), the **Taren** (the central), the **Orenam** (the south/delta fringe).
- **The Tarnesh fourth (the tribute):** **1/4 of the net pell harvest** + the **tolls at the Oren crossings**; **collected by the houses**; **of which 1/2 is remitted to the Samur treasury, 1/2 retained** (the **houses' income** → the **incentive to collect** + the **incentive to under-report** — the **evasion mechanism**, the ADM-01 §7 "chief's resistance," the TIM-02 "Tarn tribute crisis KE 490").
- **The Tarn head town (Tarenam):** the **Tarn governor** (a **low-ranking royal appointee**; a **nominal presence**; **cannot enforce** — the **negative space, NS-01: the tribute is a relationship, not an administration** — the governor is a **liaison**, not an administrator) + the **Tarenam head temple** (the 7th Veshna regional head temple, REL-02 §4 — the **religious glue**).
- **The houses' military obligation:** the **Tarn levy quota** (10–15k, the MIL-02) — the houses field the quota (the ADM-01 §2 "the chiefs provide the men, the state provides the command").

## Consequences (immediate / unintended / long-term)

- **Immediate:** the central offices (the 7 offices, the dual-elite division of labor, the hierarchy, the appointment), the legal codes (the 4 laws, the judges, the jurisdiction/appeals), the Tarn tribute governance (the 3 houses, the Tarnesh fourth, the 1/2 remittance, the Tarn governor, the Tarenam head temple, the levy quota).
- **Unintended:** the **Vorenesh's dual authority (from KE 500, the Phre governor)** is a **friction point** (the SAMUR port law vs. the Phre enclave law — the TIM-03 delta friction); the **Beshara Code's contracts/weights** are the **monetization's administrative arm** (the Beshara assesses in silver — the peasant must sell, ECO-02); the **Tarn governor's non-enforcement** means the **Tarn's loyalty is a function of the tribute terms** (when the terms worsen — the Dhresh duty, TIM-02 — the Tarn under-reports and then rebels — the Q-003 fragmentation site).
- **Long-term:** the **7 offices are the successor states' templates** (the Tarn Confederation inherits the Tarn governor's office, the Sareth Dhaneshra inherits the Sareth Dhresh, the Kesra Dhaneshra inherits the Shreshtha/Beshara/Dhresh — the TIM-03 successor states are **administrative inheritances**, not just territorial fragments); the **port law's Phre extraterritoriality** is the **post-fragmentation legal precedent** (the Phre enclaves' legal autonomy, Q-079).
