# Taxonomy of AI Fictional Tells — Master Index

Every tell documented here has: name, definition, example pattern, observable
characteristics, evidence (source IDs), likely cause (K-codes, see
`../research/01-research-synthesis.md` §1.3), cross-model / genre / perspective / length
variation, severity, false-positive risk, effect on literary quality, recommended
mitigation, side effects, and validation method. Cluster files also list
"monitored/folklore" patterns that are **not** acted upon and why.

Confidence: **H** High · **M** Medium · **L** Low (see `../research/02-evidence-hierarchy.md`).
Severity (how strongly the tell contributes to the AI-fiction feel): **3** defining ·
**2** noticeable · **1** minor. False-positive risk (how easily a *human* literary choice
is mistaken for the tell): **3** high · **2** moderate · **1** low.

## Reading a taxonomy entry

Each entry's fields:

- **Definition** — what the tell is, in behavioral terms.
- **Example pattern** — a schematic example (not a banned-word list).
- **Observable characteristics** — what an analyzer can actually look for.
- **Evidence** — source IDs; tier; confidence.
- **Likely cause** — K-codes with one-line mechanism.
- **Variation** — across models / genre / perspective / length.
- **Severity** — 1–3. **False-positive risk** — 1–3.
- **Effect on literary quality** — what the tell actually costs the story.
- **Recommended mitigation** — the minimal causal fix (mapped to intervention levels,
  see `../interventions/01-intervention-hierarchy.md`).
- **Potential side effects** — what can go wrong with the fix.
- **Validation method** — how to verify the fix helped (not just changed) the text.

**Rules.** (1) A tell may be flagged only when the *pattern* is present, never from a
word list. (2) Tells share causes; multiple flags with one cause are one intervention
problem, not many. (3) Low-confidence tells are detection-only; they cannot justify an
edit above Level 2. (4) A flagged pattern that passes the intentional-choice test (see
`../interventions/02-preservation-constraints.md` §Intentionality) is preserved, not fixed.
