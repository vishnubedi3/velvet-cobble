# Q-070 The specific names of the Tarn/Sareth/Khor chiefs

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-03 §3/§4 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the specific names of the Tarn/Sareth/Khor chiefs (the individual chiefs, their KE dates, their houses)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the DYN-01 §5 fixed the "the chiefs" but not the specific names.
- Evidence/reasoning: the DYN-01 §5 "the chiefs" + the DYN-02 §1 name pool + the DYN-03 §1/§3/§4/§6 houses.
- Alternatives considered: unnamed chiefs (rejected — the DYN-02 §1 name pool is the canon name pool); a single chief (rejected — the DYN-03 houses are the named chiefs).
- Selected resolution: the **Tarn houses** — the Keshath house (the head: the Keshath I, the Kesra I's son-in-law), the Taren house (the head: the Taren I), the Orenam house (the head: the Orenam I); the **bār chief districts** — the ~40 minor bār chiefs (the key: the Dhresham house (the Besra district), the Orenesh house (the canal district)); the **Sareth garrison chiefs** — the Threnath house (the Threna garrison command), the Gheshar house (the Gheshar); the **Khor clans** — the Kheshur, the Ghoranur, the Zhanur, the Marenur (the DYN-03 §6). The individual era-heads are a TIM-03/narrative detail, not a structural question.
- Why chosen: it is the DYN-01 §5 "the chiefs" + the DYN-02 §1 name pool made nominally/houselyl operationally operational, with the DYN-03 houses.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the DYN-03 §3/§4/§6, the DYN-02 §1 (the name pool), the TIM-03 (the chief dates).
- Canon status: CANON (DYN-03 §3/§4/§6).
- Relevant research: the comparative (the chief names — the mechanism, not a transplant).
- Recovery point: recovery/pre-question-resolutions (e48d9a3).
