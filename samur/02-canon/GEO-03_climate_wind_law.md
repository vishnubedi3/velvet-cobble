> GEO-03 fixes the **wind law** — the regime of the seasonal wind (resolves Q-004). It is a foundational world law: the Kesra calendar, the bār/pell agriculture, the military calendar, and the famine/epidemic cycles all derive from it (GEO-01 §7, TIM-01).

## 1. The two wind systems

- **The sea-wind (the wet season):** from the south/southwest, off the sea onto the land. It arrives **with the first sea-wind**, which **opens the Kesra year** (TIM-01). It lasts the **first half of the year (months 1–6)**: rain falls first on the **pell coast/delta**, then moves up the Oren; the Oren runs **high (flood stage) in months 2–6**.
- **The land-wind (the dry season):** from the interior (north/northeast), dry and dusty, **months 7–12**: firm ground, the Oren at **low stage (limited navigation)**, the campaign season (MIL-01 §4).

## 2. The wind regime (resolves Q-004)

The sea-wind's strength varies year to year. The canonical regime:

| Wind year | Frequency | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Strong wind** | every ~5–7 years | pell boom, Oren high flood, surplus |
| **Normal wind** | most years | baseline |
| **Weak wind** | every ~3–5 years | pell stress, low Oren, strain |
| **Failed wind** | every ~7–12 years | the pell fails, Oren low, famine risk in the delta/Tarn/Veth |
| **Great failure** | every ~80–120 years | catastrophic — the Great Famine (KE 455–460, TIM-02) |

**Anchored instances (consistent with TIM-02):** the weak wind KE 398; the first significant failed wind of the overstretch KE 355; the **great failure KE 455–460** (the Great Famine KE 455–470); the failed wind of the Second Vethra War KE 362 (the famine truce, TIM-03).

## 3. The regional gradient (why the core is stable and the pell is volatile)

- **The pell coast/delta/Veth (the full force):** the pell is **wind-dependent** (the paddy needs the sea-wind rain) → the **volatile** zone (DEM-01).
- **The middle Oren / bār core (buffered):** the bār is rain-fed **plus irrigated** (the Oren canals + the tanks — ECO-02); the irrigation is the **buffer** that makes the **fiscal base stable** in failed-wind years (the canal works are most critical then).
- **The Khor steppe (its own climate):** continental, thin; **independent of the sea-wind**; its own **bad-grass year every ~7–10 years** (the pastoral cycle — the Khor's internal stressor, FOR-01).

## 4. The causal chain (the world law made operational)

**Failed wind → the pell fails → the delta/Tarn/Veth famine → the debt (the moneylender) → the labor flows to the bār core (the core is spared but takes the refugees) → the epidemic risk (DEM-02) + the tax base (the pell) collapses → the revenue shock → the revolt (if the targets are held, TIM-02).**

This is the **famine → debt → revolt chain** (GEO-01 §8.4, DEM-01 §4) with its **climatological driver** fixed.

## 5. The Oren hydrology (the logistics calendar)

- **High water: months 2–6** (flood + good navigation — the wet-season river logistics, MIL-01 §4).
- **Low water: months 9–12** (limited navigation — the dry-season, the land campaign season).
- The **military calendar** (MIL-01) and the **fiscal calendar** (the tax at the harvest, TIM-01) are both set by this.

## 6. The geopolitical wind law (the shared vulnerability)

- **The Vethra is on the same sea-wind** (the Veth shore is in the wind's path, GEO-01) → the Vethra is **also wind-dependent** (its irrigation/pell + its pepper — ECO-02 §pepper).
- **The famine truce:** a failed-wind year **pauses the Vethra wars** (both sides starve) — a standing pattern (the Second Vethra War's truce, KE 362, TIM-03). Wars are fought in normal/strong-wind years.
- **The pepper spike:** a failed wind → the pepper fails → the price spikes (the Vethra's fiscal shock, ECO-02).

## Consequences (immediate / unintended / long-term)

- **Immediate:** the wind regime (the frequencies, the anchored instances), the regional gradient (the stable core, the volatile pell, the independent Khor), the causal chain (the climate-driven famine → debt → revolt), the Oren hydrology (the logistics calendar), the geopolitical wind law (the shared vulnerability, the famine truce).
- **Unintended:** the **bār core's stability is an artifact of the irrigation buffer** (ECO-02) — the core's stability *depends on* the canal works (a maintenance obligation, a corvée obligation); the **famine truce** means the Vethra wars are **seasonally/climatically gated** (a pattern the Phre later exploits — it does not share the wind dependence in the same way, TIM-03).
- **Long-term:** the **great-failure cycle (~80–120 years)** is the **environmental root cause** cadence of the Stress (TIM-01 root cause #3); the **shared wind dependence** of the Samur and the Vethra is a **structural limit on the rivalry** (they cannot destroy each other in a failed-wind year) — a negative-space mechanism (NS-01).
