# Q-004 Seasonal wind variability

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01 (§7); will constrain DEM-01 (famine/epidemic cycle), ECO-01 (tax assessment), MIL-01 (campaign season reliability)
Why it matters: a failed wind is the empire's recurring natural shock — it drives the famine → debt → revolt chain and makes revenue targets politically dangerous. Its frequency and severity are a core parameter of the imperial lifecycle.
Current best guesses: a failed or weak wind every ~5–15 years (a plausible pre-industrial range), with a catastrophic failure once in a generation or two; to be fixed once DEM-01 and the TIM timeline exist (the failures must be dated events on the timeline, not an abstract rate).
