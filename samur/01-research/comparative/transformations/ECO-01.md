# Transformation Log — ECO-01 (The Economy)

Date: 2026-08-26
Canon target: **ECO-01** (the bār/pell/cash-crop agricultural base, the silver standard/dual currency/frontier silver supply, the trade system — Oren/passes/Khor/delta/Veth/long-distance, the state monopoly — salt/horses/pepper, the merchant/banker — shahukar/war finance/growth imperative, the craft/industrialization — guilds/workshops/court as engine, who benefits/who resists)
Method: the 5-step sequential reasoning.

## Step 1 — Historical examples and underlying mechanisms

- **Mughal** — the **cash-revenue state**, the **state monopoly** (the horses, the textiles, the salt), the **transit/market dues**, the **monetization chain** (the cash crop → the moneylender → the debt → the revolt), the **state as the largest purchaser/employer**.
- **Maratha** — the **shahukar** (the merchant-banker financing the war), the **growth imperative** (the debt-financed war must keep conquering), the **cloth export**.
- **Mysore** — the **court as the economic engine** (the state demand as the market anchor), the **state workshops** (the karkhanas), the **craft-guild urban economy**, the **state-directed industrialization** (the military demand + the court demand).
- **Vijayanagara** — the **temple economy**, the **pepper/iron/horse trade**, the **coastal trade polities**.

## Step 2 — Original historical conditions

- The Mughal cash-revenue state + the state monopoly + the monetization chain.
- The Maratha shahukar + the growth imperative + the cloth export.
- The Mysore court + the workshops + the craft guilds + the industrialization.
- The Vijayanagara temple economy + the pepper/iron/horse trade.

## Step 3 — Equivalent or contrasting Samur conditions

- The Samur agricultural base is the **bār (the stable, the tax grain) + the pell (the volatile, the export) + the cash crops (the social fault line)**.
- The Samur currency is the **silver standard** (the dual currency: the silver coin + the grain), the **silver supply from the frontier** (a failed frontier = a monetary crisis).
- The Samur trade system is the **Oren (the internal artery) + the Sareth passes (the western trade) + the Khor horse trade (the strategic lifeline) + the delta/pepper (the maritime trade) + the Veth coast (the pepper) + the long-distance (the cloth, the pepper)**.
- The Samur state monopoly is the **salt, the horses, the pepper** (monopolized) + the **textiles, the transit/market dues** (taxed).
- The Samur merchant/banker is the **shahukar** (the war finance, the growth imperative, the debt).
- The Samur craft/industrialization is the **craft guilds + the state workshops (the dual-demand: the military + the court)**, the **court as the market anchor**.

**Contrasts (the load-bearing differences):** the **silver supply is from the frontier** (a failed frontier = a monetary crisis, unlike the Mughal where the silver is more internal); the **dual-demand industrialization** (the military + the court, the Mysore logic but with the military demand prominent given the three-front condition); the **Khor horse trade as a strategic lifeline** (the cavalry depends on the Khor — the horse-poor core, GEO-01 §5).

## Step 4 — Original Samur solution

The **Samur economy** — the bār/pell/cash-crop agricultural base; the silver standard (the dual currency, the frontier silver supply); the trade system (the Oren, the passes, the Khor, the delta, the Veth, the long-distance); the state monopoly (the salt, the horses, the pepper); the merchant/banker (the shahukar, the war finance, the growth imperative); the craft/industrialization (the guilds, the workshops, the court as engine) — is the **Samur-specific answer** to the **Samur conditions** (the Oren spine, the Sareth wall, the Khor steppe, the Veth coast, the Tarn plain; the frontier silver; the horse-poor core; the three-front condition). It is **not a reskin**: the **frontier silver supply** (a failed frontier = a monetary crisis), the **dual-demand industrialization** (the military + the court), and the **Khor horse trade as a strategic lifeline** are the load-bearing differences.

## Step 5 — Consequences

- **Immediate:** the agricultural base, the currency, the trade system, the state monopoly, the merchant/banker, the craft/industrialization, who benefits/who resists.
- **Unintended:** the monetization chain is the social fault line (the revolt); the growth imperative is a root cause of the overstretch; the silver supply ties the economy to the frontier (a failed frontier = a monetary crisis); the court as market anchor (the economic center of gravity); the Phre captures the maritime trade (the center of gravity shifts).
- **Long-term:** the economic contest is a root cause of the fiscal stress (TIM-01); the monetization chain is the social fault line (DEM-01); the growth imperative is the overstretch (TIM-01); the Phre's capture of Voren is the economic trigger of the fragmentation (TIM-01).
