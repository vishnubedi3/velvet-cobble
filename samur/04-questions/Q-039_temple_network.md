# Q-039 The specific temple network

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: REL-01 (§2); constrains the later REL/ECO files, the temple economy
Why it matters: the specific temple network (the Veshna temple + the regional temples — the number, the size, the land grants) defines the temple economy (the fiscal sink, the parallel landholding) and the matha's reach — the temple network is the Veshna faith's physical infrastructure and the tax-exempt parallel fiscal base (ADM-01 §7).
Current best guesses: the **Veshna temple** (the single sacred center, the great temple, GEO-02 §2) + a **network of regional temples** (the core, the Sareth, the Khor, the delta, the Veth) — the **number** (dozens to hundreds), the **size** (the Veshna great temple is the largest), the **land grants** (the devanama, the fiscal sink) — the **specific network** is to be fixed (the later REL/ECO files).
