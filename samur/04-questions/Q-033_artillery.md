# Q-033 The specific artillery

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: MIL-01 (§3); constrains TEC-01 (the industrialization, the metallurgy)
Why it matters: the specific artillery (the guns, the rockets — the state workshops, the Mysore logic — the scale, the metallurgy) defines the modernizing force and the military demand (the steel, the guns for the frontier) — the dual-demand industrialization (the military + the court) is a load-bearing feature of the Samur economy (ECO-01 §6).
Current best guesses: **state-workshop artillery** (the karkhanas, the Mysore logic) — the **guns** (the bronze/steel) + the **rockets** (the Mysore rocket logic) — the **military demand** (the guns for the frontier) + the **court demand** (the display) drive the **metallurgy** — the **scale** and the **metallurgy** are to be fixed (TEC-01).
