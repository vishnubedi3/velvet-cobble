# Q-057 The specific commanders (the Shreshthas, the frontier generals)

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-02 §3 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-02 (all eras), MIL-01 (§7), DYN-01 (§5); DYN-02 (§3); TIM-03+, the later MIL/DYN files
Why it mattered: the specific commanders (the Shreshthas, the frontier generals, the military houses) are the agents of the military events.
**Resolution (now canon, DYN-02 §3):** the key Shreshthas — **Gheshar I** (the Founding Shreshtha, KE 0–40, Kesra I's general), **Voren I** (the Dominating Shreshtha, KE ~480–520, dominates the weak king Maren I), **Besra I** (the Crisis Shreshtha, KE ~520–580, backs Threna I in the Great Succession Crisis). The Shreshtha is a salaried, non-hereditary office (different individuals, not a single family). The specific frontier generals are Q-070.
