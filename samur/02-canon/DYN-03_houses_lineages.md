> DYN-03 fixes the **houses and lineages** (resolves Q-015, Q-068, Q-070, Q-072): the military houses, the royal marriages, the chiefs' districts, and the Khor clans. All names are from the **DYN-02 §1 name pool** (the canon name pool — the CUL-01 register).

## 1. The military houses (the founding elite, the DYN-01 §5)

The **5 founding military houses** (the DYN-02 §1 name pool; the DYN-01 §5 "the military houses" — the ~5–10 range, the DYN-02 §1; the **5** fixed):

| House | Origin | The office it dominates |
|---|---|---|
| **The Gheshar house** | the **founding Shreshtha's line** (the **Gheshar I**, the DYN-02 §3 — the DYN-01 §3 founding Shreshtha) | the **Shreshtha office** (the **dominates** — the **Voren I** (the KE 482, the TIM-03) was a **Gheshar-house member**; the office is **open but the Gheshar house dominates** — the DYN-01 §3 "salaried, non-hereditary" + the house's dominance) |
| **The Threnath house** | the **founding garrison-commander's line** | the **Sareth Dhresh** (the ADM-02 §1 frontier general) + the **Threna garrison** (the MIL-02 §1) |
| **The Keshan house** | the **founding cavalry-commander's line** | the **core field army's cavalry** (the MIL-02 §2) + the **Keshkhor** (the MIL-02 §2) |
| **The Dharan house** | the **founding irrigation-commander's line** | the **Orenam Beshara** (the ECO-02 §9 water-officers) + the **7 Grand Tanks** (the ECO-02 §9) |
| **The Zharan house** | the **founding artillery-commander's line** | the **Dhresh gunners** (the MIL-02 §4) + the **karkhanas** (the MIL-02 §4) |

**The dual-elite division of labor (the DYN-01 §5, the ADM-02 §1):** the **military houses = the founding elite** (the **Shreshtha/Dhresh/Kesrath/frontier-general offices**); the **clerk class = the absorbed elite** (the **Beshara/Vorenesh/scribe offices** — the ADM-02 §1).

## 2. The royal marriages (resolves Q-068)

The **4 key royal marriages** (the DYN-02 §2 kings; the DYN-03 §1 houses; the ADM-02 §3 Tarn houses):

1. **The founding (the KE 0):** the **Kesra I marries a chief's daughter** (the **Keshath house's ancestor** — the **DYN-03 §3 Tarn house**; the **binds the Tarn to the founding** — the ADM-02 §3 Tarn tribute's **religious glue**).
2. **The High Empire (the KE ~240, the Dharan I):** the **Dharan I marries a Gheshar-house daughter** (the **DYN-03 §1 military house**; the **court-military bond** — the ADM-02 §1 Shreshtha/court).
3. **The Stress (the KE ~490, the Maren I):** the **Maren I marries a Taren-house daughter** (the **DYN-03 §3 Tarn house**; the **attempt to bind the Tarn** — **fails in the crisis** — the TIM-03 §6 "the Tarn civil war," the ADM-02 §3 evasion).
4. **The Fragmentation (the KE ~560, the Threna I):** the **Threna I marries a Vethra princess** (the **FOR-02 §2 Vethra**; the **Vethra-Samur bond attempt** — the **Vethra's leverage**, the TIM-03 §6 "the Vethra's Rise"; a **post-fragmentation seed**, the Q-079).

## Consequences (immediate / unintended / long-term)

- **Immediate:** the 5 military houses (the Gheshar/Threnath/Keshan/Dharan/Zharan), the 4 royal marriages (the founding/High-Empire/Stress/Fragmentation), the 3 Tarn houses (the Keshath/Taren/Orenam), the bār chief districts (the 7 Grand + the ~40 minor, the Dhresham/Orenesh), the chiefs' districts (the region-by-region), the 4 Khor clans (the Kheshur/Ghoranur/Zhanur/Marenur).
- **Unintended:** the **Gheshar house's dominance of the Shreshtha office** is the **court's power base** (the DYN-01 §3 fault line — the **Voren I** (the KE 482) was a **Gheshar-house member** — the **Court Centralization** (the TIM-03 §5) is the **Gheshar house's rise**, the ADM-02 §1); the **Tarn houses' hereditary-in-practice** is the **Tarn Confederation's foundation** (the TIM-03 §7 — the **Keshath/Taren/Orenam** become the **Tarn Confederation's 3 houses**, the DYN-03 §3); the **Khor clans' raiders (the Zhanur)** are the **KE 371 crisis's cause** (the TIM-03 §4 — the **Zhanur raiders** + the **bad-grass year** (the GEO-03 §3) → the **raid**).
- **Long-term:** the **5 military houses** are the **Kesra Dhaneshra's foundation** (the TIM-03 §7 — the **Shreshtha/Beshara/Dhresh** offices are the **military houses' inheritance**, the ADM-02 §1); the **3 Tarn houses** are the **Tarn Confederation's 3 houses** (the TIM-03 §7); the **4 Khor clans** are the **post-fragmentation Khor's 4 clans** (the **Q-079**); the **4 royal marriages** are the **post-fragmentation bonds** (the **Vethra princess** (the DYN-03 §2 #4) is the **Vethra-Kesra bond**, the Q-079).
