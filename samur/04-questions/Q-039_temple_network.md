Status: RESOLVED → REL-02 §4 (2026-08-26)

Original question: the specific temple network (the Veshna temple + the regional temples — the number, the size, the land grants)?

**Resolution record**

- Evidence/reasoning: the Sanatan temple economy (the devanama, the parallel landholding) + the GEO-02 cities (the 7 head temples) + the REL-01 §2 "the 10–15% temple land."

- Why chosen: it is the Sanatan temple economy + the GEO-02 cities made nominally/tieredly operationally operational, with the mobile node as the REL-02 §4 portability.
