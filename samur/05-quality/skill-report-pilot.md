### Notable passes (no finding)

- **P01 (modal-average phrasing):** the prose uses story-specific vocabulary (devanama, frozen orthography, Shastras, conciliar hall, Tarnesh Dawn). No generic texture.
- **P04 (uniform sentence rhythm):** sentence length variance is strong. Fragments ("Cold. The same cold."), long compound sentences, short declaratives, dialogue exchanges all coexist.
- **P05 ("as if" scaffolding):** ~5 similes across ~5,200 words; within genre baseline. Each is grounded in the POV character's experience domain. Zero "as if" constructions.
- **N02 (explicit thematic statement):** thematic statements are character-embedded ("There was no king. The grammar had a subject with no one to fill it." — Thesra's interiority, not narrator-imposed).
- **N06 (neat closure):** the chapter ends without resolution. Thesra's awareness shifts but nothing is resolved.
- **N08 (valence-smoothed emotional arc):** the wall scene (pressing palm against stone, twenty-eight years, inarticulate anger) provides a genuine negative beat. The arc has texture.
- **C03 (uniform articulate interiority):** Thesra's interiority is restrained (images, incomplete thoughts); Vesharan's is shorter and more hedging; the guru's interiority is not shown.
- **D04 (uniform idiolect):** five distinct voices (junior priest, guru, Kheshan, Vesharan, Thesra) with differentiated register, sentence length, and physical business.
- **SC06 (lack of incidental behavior):** junior priest shifts the broom; Threna head arranges objects; Vesharan taps the manuscript; Kheshan steadies himself on wet stone; a guard adjusts a strap.
- **E05 (positivity smoothing):** the wall scene is a genuine negative beat, unredeemed within the scene.
- **S01 (atmosphere-first opening):** the opening is an event ("The water came up through the old channel before the priests did."), not a mood-setting description.
