# Q-026 The minting system

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ECO-01 (§2); constrains the later ECO files, the currency
Why it matters: the specific minting system (the mints, the coin denominations, the silver content, the counterfeiting) defines the currency's stability and the state's monetary control — the silver content and the counterfeiting are sources of the monetary crisis (a failed frontier = a silver shortage, ECO-01 §2).
Current best guesses: **five mints** (Kesra, Besra, Voren, Threna, Keshkhor — the major cities, GEO-02), a **silver coin of a standard weight** (the "unit" of the silver standard) + **fractional coins** (the smaller denominations) + the **grain** as the tax-in-kind; the **silver content is fixed** (the standard) but **counterfeiting** (the debasement) is a problem in the Stress (the monetary crisis, TIM-01).
