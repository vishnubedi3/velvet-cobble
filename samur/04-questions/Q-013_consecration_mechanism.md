# Q-013 The temple's consecration mechanism

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-01 (§3, §4, §6); will constrain REL-01 (the religion), the succession
Why it matters: the consecration is what makes a king "legitimate" — its exact ritual, its reversibility (de-legitimation), and who performs it (the head of the Veshna matha) define the temple's real power as kingmaker and the fault line of the composite legitimacy.
Current best guesses: a **ritual investiture + a public oath before the temple**, performed by the **head of the Veshna matha** (the guru); **reversible in principle** (a king can be de-legitimized — a "de-consecration" — by the temple acting with the house's consent), but **rarely used** (a last resort, because de-legitimating a king is itself a legitimacy crisis).
