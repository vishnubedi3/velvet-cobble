# Q-085 The pre-imperial named agents and polity internals

Status: OPEN (a narrative detail)
Date: 2026-08-28
Related: TIM-05 (the Pre-Kesra Age — §10 the five-layer memory distinction, §12 what is uncertain), DYN-02 §1 (the personal-name pool), CUL-01 (the naming convention — place names double as personal names)
Why it matters: the pre-imperial **lines, houses, clans, and institutions are canon** (the Orenar line — the temple city's hereditary rulers, the House of Kesra's ancestry; the Samur protector line; the Sarethan hill-people — the Sareth highlands' conservative Orenic hill register; the 3 Tarn houses' chiefdoms; the 4 Khor clans; the Orenar succession practice; the union marriage's line-level fact). The **named agents** (the individual names of the pre-imperial rulers; the Orenar line's members; the Sarethan hill chiefdoms' internals; the Veth ports' pre-state details) are a **narrative-stage texture** — a narrative detail, like the Q-081/Q-082. **Constraint:** if ever used, the names must come from the **DYN-02 §1 name pool** (the canon name pool — the CUL-01 register), and the individuals must remain consistent with the line-level canon (the TIM-05 §4–§7) and the five-layer memory distinction (the individuals are **forgotten in the world's record** — a narrative that names them is a narrative act, not a record claim).
