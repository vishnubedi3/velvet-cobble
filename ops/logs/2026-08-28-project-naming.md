## The Decision

- **Project Name:** The Wind's Record
- **Codename:** WINDLAW

## Rationale

### Why "The Wind's Record"

The project's identity, as it has developed, is defined by the world's deepest truths:

1. **The wind is the foundational law** — it governs agriculture, military campaigns, fiscal revenue, and the rise and fall of civilizations (GEO-03).
2. **The sea is the world's oldest continuous life** — billions of years deep (TIM-09).
3. **"The stone remembers the sea"** — the world's deepest formula (TIM-09 §9).
4. **The old land lies under the new** — the landmasses have been reassembled (TIM-10 §9).
5. **The world's chronology is deeper than its civilizations** — billions → millions → hundreds of thousands → tens of thousands → thousands (TIM-07).

Everything in this world — every civilization, every famine, every empire, every night — is written by the wind's cadence. The project itself is a record: the deep chronology, the canon, the novel. "Record" works as both noun (the chronicle) and verb (the act of recording). The civilizations that rose and fell are the wind's record. The stone that remembers the sea is the wind's record. The novel is the wind's record.

The name is not derived from "Sumur Empire" or any single element within the world. It encompasses the entire project — the planet's formation, the ocean's establishment, the landmasses' drift, the wind's regime, the deep civilizations, the pre-imperial history, the six epochs, and the novel.

### Why "WINDLAW"

The wind law is the world's foundational principle — the one invariant that governs everything from the planet's atmospheric circulation to the rise and fall of empires. As a codename, it is:

- Short, functional, and memorable
- Distinct from any in-world term (no place, character, or institution bears this name)
- A project-level identifier, not a world-level one
- Not derived from "Sumur Empire" or any single narrative element

### What was preserved

- **"Samur Empire"** remains the name of the fictional empire within the world. It is used where the empire itself is the subject (canon files, historical analysis, the novel's setting).
- **"Veshran"** remains the name of the continent within the world.
- **"velvet-cobble"** remains the repository name (a platform-assigned identifier). The project's codename WINDLAW is the project-level identifier.
