# Q-026 The specific minting system

Status: RESOLVED → ECO-02 §3 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the specific minting system (the mints, the coin denominations, the silver content, the counterfeiting)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the ECO-01 §4 fixed the "silver standard" but not the mints/coins/fineness.
- Evidence/reasoning: the Mughal silver standard (the fineness, the debasement, the mint mark) + the GEO-02 cities (the 5 mints).
- Alternatives considered: a single mint (rejected — the 5 cities are the mints, the GEO-02); a fixed fineness (rejected — the debasement is the Stress inflation, the TIM-02).
- Selected resolution: the **5 mints** (the Kesra, the Besra, the Voren, the Threna, the Keshkhor); the coinage: the Kesra standard coin (the silver unit, named for the dynasty), the half-Kesra, the quarter-Kesra, the bāl token (the copper small-change); the fineness: the Kesra fine = 90% (the High-Empire standard, the Varan I reform KE 300); the debasement (the Stress, the Zharan I: KE 435: 85% → KE 460: 75%); the counterfeiting (the criminal + the Tarn forgeries); the mint mark (each mint's mark, the Kesra mint = the Oren-wave).
- Why chosen: it is the Mughal silver standard + the GEO-02 cities made nominally operationally operational, with the debasement as the TIM-02 Stress inflation.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the ECO-02 §3, the TIM-03 (the KE 300/435/460), the ADM-02 §1 (the Beshara's mint oversight).
- Canon status: CANON (ECO-02 §3).
- Relevant research: the comparative `mughal.md` (the silver standard).
