# Q-080 The Empty Throne's filling

Status: OPEN (the next development unit)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-04 §7/§8 (the four claims, the Empty Throne, the Consecration Moratorium), REL-02 §3/§8 (the consecration rite, the de-consecrations)
Why it matters: the Empty Throne (the protector-of-dharma title, empty since the Consecration Moratorium, post-KE ~700) is the Dhaneshra Period's central open political question — which of the four claims (the Temple Line; the Keshath; the Gheshar; the Threnath) will eventually be consecrated (or the Empty Throne persists). The filling of the Empty Throne is the Dhaneshra Period's open political question (the next development unit).
Current best guesses: the four claims (the DYN-04 §7) — the Temple Line (the true line, sheltered at the Great Temple); the Keshath (the founding-marriage descendant, recorded but not recognized); the Gheshar (the founding Shreshtha's line, the office); the Threnath (the defender claim). The Empty Throne can be filled by any of them — the succession question is the Dhaneshra Period's open political question. To be fixed in the next development unit.
