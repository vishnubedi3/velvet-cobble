**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the MIL-01 §4 fixed the "Khor horse dependency" but not the quota/quality/terms.
- Evidence/reasoning: the Maratha horse-trade mechanism + the GEO-01 §5 "the Khor steppe" + the DYN-03 §6 Khor clans.
- Alternatives considered: a domestic horse breed (rejected — the GEO-01 §5 "the horse-poor core"); a single Khor clan (rejected — the DYN-03 §6 4 clans).

- Why chosen: it is the Maratha horse-trade + the GEO-01 §5 scarcity made nominally/quantitatively operationally operational, with the dependency as the MIL-01 §4 strategic vulnerability.
