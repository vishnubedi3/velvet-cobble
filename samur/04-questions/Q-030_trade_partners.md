# Q-030 The long-distance trade partners

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ECO-01 (§3); constrains FOR-01 (the trade, the Phre)
Why it matters: the specific long-distance trade partners (the Samur cloth to the wider world, the pepper to the maritime partners — who are the partners) define the external economic relationships and the Phre's entry — the Phre (the maritime power) enters via the delta (Voren) and captures the maritime trade (the economic trigger of the fragmentation, TIM-01).
Current best guesses: the **maritime partners** (the Phre — the distant maritime power, the chartered company; the Vethra — the "state too strong" to the south; the Khor horse-people — the northern partners) + the **wider world** (the long-distance cloth/pepper trade) — the **specific partners** and the **trade terms** are to be fixed (FOR-01).
