# Q-009 Urban vs. rural split

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DEM-01; will constrain CUL-01 (the city culture), ECO-01 (the urban economy)
Why it matters: the share of the population in cities (vs. villages) sets the scale of the urban economy, the market system, and the social structure (the merchant/artisan classes vs. the agrarian majority).
Current best guesses: a **pre-industrial rural majority** — roughly **10–20% urban** (the cities + the market towns), the rest rural — consistent with the dense agrarian core + the port/market cities.
