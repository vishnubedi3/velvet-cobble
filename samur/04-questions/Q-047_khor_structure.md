# Q-047 The specific Khor structure

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: FOR-01 (§3); constrains the later FOR/TIM files, the Khor campaigns
Why it matters: the specific Khor structure (the horse peoples — the number, the names, the seasonal assembly, the leaders) defines the steppe's political structure and the horse trade — the Khor's horse dependency is the Samur's strategic vulnerability (MIL-01 §4).
Current best guesses: **multiple horse peoples** (the steppe — the number, the names) + the **seasonal assembly** (the loose confederation, the trade/raiding/marriage) + the **leaders** (the horse peoples' chiefs, the assembly's leaders) — the **specific structure** is to be fixed (the later FOR/TIM files).
