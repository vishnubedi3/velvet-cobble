# Q-079 The post-fragmentation matha politics

Status: OPEN (a seed for the post-fragmentation work)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: REL-02 §4/§8 (the matha's portability, the de-consecrations), TIM-03 §7 (the successor states), CUL-02 §1 (the language map)
Why it matters: the four polities (the Tarn Confederation, the Sareth Dhaneshra, the Kesra Dhaneshra, the Vethra) recognize the Veshna matha differently; the Phre enclaves' relation to it is open. The matha is the one institution that survives all five eras intact (the REL-02 §4) — its post-fragmentation politics is the post-fragmentation's religious seed.
Current best guesses: the Kesra Dhaneshra (the rump that claims the Kesra legitimacy) recognizes the matha as the apex; the Tarn Confederation recognizes it as the 7th head temple's authority; the Sareth Dhaneshra recognizes it as a neighbor; the Vethra recognizes it as a cousin (the Veth faith as a Veshna variant, the REL-02 §6); the Phre enclaves treat it as a foreign power (the sea-script vs. the Veshna sacred script, the CUL-02).
