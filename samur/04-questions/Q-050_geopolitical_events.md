# Q-050 The specific geopolitical events

Status: RESOLVED → FOR-02 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: FOR-01 (§5), TIM-02, FOR-02; the foreign policy
Why it mattered: the specific geopolitical events (the Vethra wars, the Khor campaigns, the Phre's capture of Voren — the dates, the commanders, the outcomes) are the spine of the foreign policy.
**Resolution (now canon, FOR-02):** the specific geopolitical events are now anchored to the named foreign rulers — the Vethra wars (Pelan I, the First War Ruler, ~220–300), the Khor campaigns (Ghoran Ur, the Trade Leader, ~180–300; Zhan Ur, the Frontier Leader, ~300–450), the Phre's capture of Voren (Phrean, the First Company Governor, ~450–520). The specific dates/commanders/outcomes are Q-071/Q-072/Q-073.
