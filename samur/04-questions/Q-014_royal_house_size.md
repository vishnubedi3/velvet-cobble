# Q-014 Size and composition of the royal house

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-01 (§4, §5); will constrain the succession contest, DYN-02
Why it matters: the number of royal agnates (claimants) at any time sets the size of the succession contest — a large house means many claimants (more factional competition, the Mughal pattern); a small house means fewer claimants (more dynastic fragility, the succession can fail).
Current best guesses: a **medium-sized house** — roughly **10–30 senior agnates** at any time (the founding Samur warrior band's kin, plus the absorbed royal-line marriages), enough for a **real contest** (the "eldest vs. the most worthy" + the three-way) but not so many that the succession is unmanageable.
