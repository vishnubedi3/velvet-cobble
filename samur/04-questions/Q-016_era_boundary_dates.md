# Q-016 Exact era-boundary dates

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-01 (§2, §3); constrains all TIM-02+ event dating
Why it matters: the era boundaries (currently era-typical ranges) must be fixed as the specific anchor events are dated — the boundaries are defined *by* the anchor events, not the other way around.
Current best guesses: the boundaries will snap to the nearest anchor event (e.g., the Founding/Expansion boundary to the end of the first succession ~KE 60; the High Empire/Stress boundary to the first major overstretch crisis ~KE 380; the Stress/Fragmentation boundary to the Phre's first delta enclave ~KE 500).
