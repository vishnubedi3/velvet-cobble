# Q-007 Ethnic and linguistic composition of the regions

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01, GEO-02; will constrain CUL-01 (the languages/dialects), DYN-01 (the legitimacy), FOR-01 (the neighbors)
Why it matters: the empire's regions are different in kind (the bār core, the pell coast, the tribute east, the steppe, the coast) — so they are likely **different in people and language** too. This shapes the legitimacy claim (who the dynasty "represents"), the administrative language, and the fault lines of the later fragmentation.
Current best guesses: a **core Samur language** (the court/administrative language of the bār core) + a **coastal trade pidgin** (the delta/Veth) + a **steppe language** (the Khor) + the **Tarn plain's own language(s)** (the tributary east) — i.e., a **multi-lingual empire** with a dominant administrative language, not a monolingual one.
