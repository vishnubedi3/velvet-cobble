# Q-022 The specific tax rates

Status: RESOLVED → ECO-02 §1 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the specific tax rates (the bār grain rate, the pell rate, the transit/market dues, the tribute rate)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the ADM-01 §3 fixed the monetization chain but not the rates.
- Evidence/reasoning: the Mughal zabt mechanism (the 1/4 land tax) + the Maratha chauth (the 1/4 tribute) + the ADM-01 §7 "the revenue targets inflate" (the Stress inflation).
- Alternatives considered: a higher rate (rejected — the 1/4 is the Mughal zabt standard, the pre-industrial ceiling); a lower (rejected — the 1/4 is the fiscal base, the DEM-01 §2 "the tax base").
- Selected resolution: the **High-Empire standard rates** — the bār land tax = 1/4 of the assessed yield (the "quarter duty," in kind → in silver); the wartime levy (the "Dhresh duty") = +1/8 in the war years (the Stress-era inflation, the Peshan I reform KE 350); the pell tax = 1/5 of the yield + the port dues; the transit/market dues = 2–5%; the Tarn tribute (the Tarnesh fourth) = 1/4 of the net pell harvest + the crossings' tolls (1/2 remitted); the temple = exempt (the devanama); the monopolies = the state price (the salt/horse/pepper). The inflation mechanism (the Stress) = the Dhresh duty + the debasement + the assessment inflation.
- Why chosen: it is the Mughal zabt + the Maratha chauth made nominally operationally operational, with the Stress inflation as the TIM-02 revolt trigger.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the ECO-02 §1, the TIM-03 (the KE 350/435/460), the ADM-02 §3 (the Tarn tribute).
- Canon status: CANON (ECO-02 §1).
