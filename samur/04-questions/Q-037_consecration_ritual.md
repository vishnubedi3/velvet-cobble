# Q-037 The specific consecration ritual

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-02 §4 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: REL-01 (§3), DYN-01 (§3, §4); the succession; the Great Succession Crisis (TIM-01); DYN-02 (§4)
Why it mattered: the specific consecration ritual (the mechanism, the reversibility, the performers) defines the matha's real power as kingmaker and the fault line of the composite legitimacy.
**Resolution (now canon, DYN-02 §4):** a **ritual investiture + a public oath before the Veshna temple**, performed by the **head of the Veshna matha** (the guru) — the king is consecrated as the **protector of dharma**. **Reversible in principle** (a "de-consecration" by the matha acting with the house's consent) but **rarely used** (a last resort). The **Great Succession Crisis exception:** the matha is **split** (the sanction is withheld) — the kingmaker is divided, which is why the crisis is uncontested.
