# Q-070 The specific names of the Tarn/Sareth/Khor chiefs

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-02 (§3, §5), ADM-01 (§2), MIL-01 (§6); constrains the later DYN/ADM files
Why it matters: the specific names of the Tarn/Sareth/Khor chiefs (the individual chiefs, their KE dates, their houses) are the agents of the tributary east, the western defense, and the northern frontier.
Current best guesses: the **Tarn chiefs** (the tributary east, the fragmentation site), the **Sareth chiefs** (the western defense, the passes), the **Khor horse-people chiefs** (the seasonal assembly, the hard frontier) — the **specific chiefs** (the names, the KE dates, the houses) are to be fixed (the later DYN/ADM files).
