# Q-042 The specific Tarn language(s)

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: CUL-01 (§2); constrains FOR-01 (the tributary east), the later CUL files
Why it matters: the specific Tarn language(s) (the tributary east — the number, the languages, the relationship to the Samur language) define the cultural pluralism of the tributary east — the Tarn is the fragmentation site (the successor states), so its language(s) are the post-imperial cultural map.
