# Q-005 The nature of the Veth pepper / coastal goods

Status: RESOLVED → ECO-02 §5 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the nature of the "Veth pepper" (a single cash crop, or a range of coastal goods)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the GEO-01 §5 fixed the "pepper" as the high-value coastal good but not its nature (a single crop, a range).
- Evidence/reasoning: the Mysore/ Vijayanagara pepper-trade mechanism (the high-value-to-weight maritime good) + the GEO-03 §6 "the pepper's wind dependence" + the ECO-01 §6 court demand.
- Alternatives considered: a range of coastal goods (rejected — the monopoly logic needs a single dominant high-value good for the state control at Kesveth/Voren); a non-plant good (rejected — the "pepper" is a plant crop in the mechanism).
- Selected resolution: the **Vetham (the Veth pepper)** — a climbing shrub of the Veth hill-slopes; needs the monsoon (the sea-wind, the GEO-03) + the humid slope; small plots, high value; picked green, sun-dried/fermented (the labor-intensive cure); the dried pod, the "pepper-basket" unit; the high value-to-weight → the maritime trade; the monopoly logic (the small area, the high value, the easy state control); the wind dependence (the failed wind → the pepper fails → the price spikes, the Vethra's fiscal shock).
- Why chosen: it is the GEO-01 §5 "pepper" made botanically/economically operational, with the GEO-03 §6 wind dependence as the Vethra's fiscal shock.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: ECO-02 §2 (the pepper monopoly), GEO-03 §6 (the wind dependence), FOR-01 §2 (the Vethra's economy), TIM-03 (the pepper quota, the Treaty of Kesveth KE 208).
- Canon status: CANON (ECO-02 §5).
- Relevant research: the comparative `vijayanagara.md` (the pepper trade), the `mysore.md` (the high-value crop).
