# Q-006 City size and tiering

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-02; will constrain DEM-01 (urban density), CUL-01 (the city culture), ECO-01 (the market system)
Why it matters: the tiering of cities (one metropolis? several large towns?) shapes the urban economy, the military logistics (the garrison sizes), and the social structure of the cities.
Current best guesses: **Voren** (the delta port) and **Kesra** (the capital) are the two largest (each ~100–250k, a plausible pre-industrial range); **Besra** a large market town (~50–100k); the others smaller; a long tail of villages and market towns.
