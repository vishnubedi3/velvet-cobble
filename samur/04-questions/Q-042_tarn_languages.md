# Q-042 The specific Tarn language(s)

Status: RESOLVED → CUL-02 §4 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the specific Tarn language(s) (the number, the languages, the relationship to the Samur language)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the CUL-01 §2 fixed the "Tarn language(s)" but not the specifics.
- Evidence/reasoning: the CUL-01 §2 "the Tarn language(s)" + the DYN-03 §3 3 houses + the CUL-02 §1 Orenic family.
- Alternatives considered: a single Tarn language (rejected — the 3 houses need the 3 varieties); a non-Orenic language (rejected — the CUL-02 §1 Orenic family).
- Selected resolution: **not one language but 2–3 Orenic lowland varieties** — the Keshath variety (the north), the Taren variety (the central), the Orenam variety (the south/delta fringe); the drift: a softer Orenic drift (more vowels, fewer clusters, the -an/-am suffixes) + a local substrate; the trade register: the Samur-Tarn trade register (the tribute administration language, the ADM-02 §3).
- Why chosen: it is the CUL-01 §2 + the DYN-03 §3 3 houses made nominally/varietally operationally operational, with the trade register as the ADM-02 §3 tribute administration.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the CUL-02 §4, the DYN-03 §3 (the 3 houses), the ADM-02 §3 (the tribute administration).
- Canon status: CANON (CUL-02 §4).
- Relevant research: the comparative (the language varieties — the mechanism, not a transplant).
- Recovery point: recovery/pre-question-resolutions (e48d9a3).
- Resulting changes: CUL-02 §4 (new), the influence register (the CUL-02 row), the CHANGELOG.
