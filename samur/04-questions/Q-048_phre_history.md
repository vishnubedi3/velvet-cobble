# Q-048 The specific Phre history

Status: RESOLVED → FOR-02 §4 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: FOR-01 (§4), TIM-02, FOR-02 (§4); the Phre's capture of Voren
Why it mattered: the specific Phre history (the founding, the chartered company, the delta enclaves — the dates, the Phre's leaders, the outcomes) defines the maritime power and the Fragmentation's economic trigger.
**Resolution (now canon, FOR-02 §4):** the Phre's company leaders (the chartered company's governors) — **Phrean** (the First Company Governor, ~450–520, the founding of the chartered company, the first delta enclave at Voren), **Shoran** (the Second Company Governor, ~520–620, the Phre's paramountcy), **Phrean II** (the Third Company Governor, ~620–680, the Phre's Fragmentation). The company is **re-chartered** (the chartered company, not hereditary). The specific Phre company governors' terms are Q-073.
