# Transformation Log — TEC-01 (Technology — a Cumulative Historical Process)

Date: 2026-08-26
Canon target: **TEC-01** (the technology as a cumulative historical process — the irrigation, the metallurgy, the artillery, the craft, the logistics; each with origins, required resources, adoption resistance, and socio-economic impact; the dual demand)
Method: the 5-step sequential reasoning.

## Step 1 — Historical examples and underlying mechanisms

- **Mysore** — the **court as the economic engine** (the state demand), the **state workshops** (the karkhanas), the **craft-guilds**, the **state-directed industrialization** (the military demand + the court demand), the **metallurgy**, the **artillery** (the guns/rockets).
- **Mughal** — the **state as the largest purchaser/employer** (the state demand), the **state monopoly** (the state's commercial power).
- **Maratha** — the **professionalization** (the artillery, the fort), the **mule logistics**.

## Step 2 — Original historical conditions

- The Mysore court + the state workshops + the craft-guilds + the industrialization + the metallurgy + the artillery.
- The Mughal state demand + the state monopoly.
- The Maratha professionalization + the mule logistics.

## Step 3 — Equivalent or contrasting Samur conditions

- The technology is a **cumulative historical process** (the irrigation → the metallurgy → the artillery → the craft → the logistics, over the eras).
- Each technology has its **origins**, **required resources**, **adoption resistance**, and **socio-economic impact**.
- The **dual demand** (the military + the court) — the **load-bearing** feature (the Mysore logic).
- The **adoption resistance** (the corvée, the guilds, the frontier, the Phre) — the technology is **contested**.

**Contrasts (the load-bearing differences):** the **cumulative process** (over the eras); the **dual demand** (the military + the court); the **adoption resistance** (the corvée, the guilds, the frontier, the Phre — the technology is contested).

## Step 4 — Original Samur solution

The **Samur technology** — the cumulative historical process (the irrigation → the metallurgy → the artillery → the craft → the logistics, over the eras), each with its origins, required resources, adoption resistance, and socio-economic impact, driven by the dual demand (the military + the court) — is the **Samur-specific answer** to the **Samur conditions** (the bār core, the Sareth/southern highlands, the Oren, the delta, the five factions, the overstretch). It is **not a reskin**: the **cumulative process** (over the eras), the **dual demand** (the military + the court), and the **adoption resistance** (the corvée, the guilds, the frontier, the Phre) are the load-bearing differences.

## Step 5 — Consequences

- **Immediate:** the technology as a cumulative historical process; each technology's origins, required resources, adoption resistance, and socio-economic impact; the dual demand.
- **Unintended:** the adoption resistance (the corvée, the guilds, the frontier, the Phre) — the technology is contested; the dual demand (the military + the court) — the technology is driven by the overstretch (the military) + the market anchor (the court).
- **Long-term:** the technology is the material base of the empire's power (the state power, the military, the trade) — and its adoption resistance is a root cause of the empire's stress and fragmentation (TIM-01).
