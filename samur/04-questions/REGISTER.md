## The status taxonomy

- **Resolved** — the question is answered in canon (a pointer to the canon file/section).
- **Partially resolved** — the structural part is fixed; the rest is a deliberate range (a provisional range, pending a later refinement).
- **Intentionally unresolved** — the uncertainty is an intentional part of the world (a mystery, a hidden-history seed, a narrative-stage artifact).
- **Provisional** — a deliberate range (a subset of partially resolved).
- **Blocked by missing information** — a question that cannot be resolved yet (a dependency on an unresolved foundational question or an external input).
- **Rejected premise** — the question's premise is wrong (a bookkeeping closure).
