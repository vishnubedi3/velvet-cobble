# Q-067 The specific battles of each king

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-02 (§2), TIM-02, MIL-01 (§6, §8), FOR-01 (§5); constrains TIM-03+, the later MIL/FOR files
Why it matters: the specific battles of each king (the Vethra wars, the Khor campaigns, the Sareth defense, the delta defense — the individual battles, their KE dates, their outcomes) are the spine of the military history.
Current best guesses: the **Kharan I** battles (the First Vethra War, KE ~200–240), the **Peshan I** battles (the overstretch, the frontier), the **Ghoran I** battles (the revolt), the **Kesra III / Threna I** battles (the Great Succession Crisis, the fragmentation) — the **specific battles** are to be fixed (TIM-03+).
