# Q-037 The specific consecration ritual

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: REL-01 (§3), DYN-01 (§3, §4); resolves Q-013; constrains the succession, the Great Succession Crisis (TIM-01)
Why it matters: the specific consecration ritual (the mechanism, the reversibility, the performers) defines the matha's real power as kingmaker and the fault line of the composite legitimacy — the de-legitimation (the reversal) is the last resort that can break a succession (the Great Succession Crisis, TIM-01).
Current best guesses: a **ritual investiture + a public oath before the Veshna temple**, performed by the **head of the Veshna matha** (the guru) — **reversible in principle** (a "de-consecration" by the matha acting with the house's consent) but **rarely used** (a last resort, because de-legitimating a king is itself a legitimacy crisis) — the **specific ritual** is to be fixed (the later REL files).
