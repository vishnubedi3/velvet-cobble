# Q-023 Specific form of the state monopoly

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ADM-01 (§3); constrains ECO-01 (the trade), the revenue
Why it matters: which goods are monopolized (the salt, the horses, the textiles, the pepper) vs. taxed (the transit/market dues) determines the state's direct commercial power and its relationship to the merchant/bankers (the shahukar) — the monopoly is a source of revenue *and* of resentment (the merchant resistance, ADM-01 §7).
Current best guesses: the **state monopolizes the salt, the horses, and the pepper** (the high-value, the strategic, the frontier goods — the Mughal logic) and **taxes the textiles + the transit/market dues** (the bulk, the port trade) — the **pepper monopoly** is the most contested (the port, the merchant/bankers, the Phre all want it).
