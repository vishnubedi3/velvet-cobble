# Q-015 Initial division of the absorbed chiefs' districts

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-01 (§2, §5); will constrain ADM-01 (the provincial government), the successor states
Why it matters: how the absorbed chiefs' districts are divided (how many chiefs, how large the districts, which sit on the Oren / the Sareth / the Tarn) determines the provincial structure and the shape of the future successor states.
Current best guesses: **several dozen chiefs** (the pre-existing agrarian-military houses of the upper/mid Oren), each holding a **district in revenue assignment** with a military-service obligation; the **largest districts** sit on the **Oren (the fiscal spine)** and the **Sareth passes (the west trade)** — so the most powerful chiefs are the ones the center most needs (and most fears).
