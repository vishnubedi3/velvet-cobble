# Transformation Log — GEO-01 (Material & Geographic Foundation)

Date: 2026-08-26
Canon target: **GEO-01** (the Samur physical base: the Oren watershed, the Sareth west wall, the Khor north steppe, the Veth south coast, the Tarn east periphery)
Method: the 5-step sequential reasoning (see `comparative/README.md`).

## Step 1 — Historical examples and underlying mechanisms

- **Mughal** — a multi-front agrarian empire with **resource-scarcity** (the horse trade, salt, silver) and a **river logistics spine** (the great river).
- **Maratha** — a **defensible core** (a mountain wall) that is **easy to defend but expensive to project from** (the defensible-core / projection-problem).
- **Vijayanagara** — a **standing frontier** as a **permanent fiscal-military condition**, not a single war.
- **Qing** — a **tiered frontier** (the open steppe, **defensible only by presence**) and the **"inner colony"** (a remote, high-revenue periphery hard to administer).
- **Mysore** — a **compact, coast-adjacent state that is "too strong for its size"** and gets **contained**.
- **British** — a **distant maritime power** projecting through a **chartered trading company** from **coastal / delta enclaves**, with **naval / river logistics as the binding constraint** and **seasonal (monsoon) sea-lanes** making access **seasonal**.

## Step 2 — Original historical conditions

- **Mughal:** a fertile great-river plain (the core) + plateau/steppe (west) and plateau (south) frontiers + the horse trade + the river as the logistics spine.
- **Maratha:** a mountain wall (the defensible core) + a coast (the trade) + a thin agrarian plateau (no hereditary land-assignment possible → revenue-sharing).
- **Vijayanagara:** a plateau core on a river basin + a standing rival frontier (the sultanates) + tank/irrigation agriculture.
- **Qing:** an open steppe (the north frontier, no wall) + a settled core (the provinces) + a remote frontier with a different legal regime (the "inner colonies").
- **Mysore:** a mountain wall + a coast (the compact, irrigated, craft-guild state) + surrounding larger powers (the containment).
- **British:** an island (no land frontier) + the seasonal sea-lanes + the chartered company (the externalized cost of empire) + the delta / coast enclaves (the power-base).

## Step 3 — Equivalent or contrasting Samur conditions

- The **Samur core** = the **Oren basin** (analogous to the great-river plain core — a fertile river heartland).
- The **Sareth** = the **defensible wall** (analogous to a mountain wall, but placed **west**, running **north–south**, with the core on its **east** side).
- The **Khor** = the **open steppe frontier** (analogous to the steppe/plateau frontier — open, no wall, the horse source).
- The **Veth / Vethra** = the **compact, coast-adjacent "too big" state** (analogous to Mysore, but placed on the **south coast**, compact and **paddy/irrigated**, not a plateau state).
- The **Tarn plain / the delta** = the **remote, high-revenue periphery** (analogous to the tributary "inner colony" — but a **paddy / delta** periphery, crossed by water).
- The **Phre** = the **distant maritime / chartered power** (analogous to the maritime commercial power — but it arrives **via the Oren delta mouth**, not from adjacent land; and the empire is **not an island**, so the maritime power's reach is **bounded by the riverine reach** of the Oren).

**Contrasts (the load-bearing differences):**
- The Samur core sits **behind a west wall** (the Sareth), not on an open plain → the **projection problem is to the west / north**, not in all directions.
- The **horse frontier is to the north** (the Khor), not to the west (as in the Mughal, where the western/Deccan horse pressure is the main one).
- The **"too big" neighbor is a compact coastal state to the south** (Vethra), not a large interior state → the containment is a **coastal / delta** rivalry, not an interior war.
- The **maritime power arrives via the delta** (the Oren mouth), not from adjacent land → its reach is **bounded by the riverine reach** (it holds the delta mouth + the ports, but not the interior).
- The **three most scarce resources (horses, timber/iron, silver) are on several different borders** (north; west; north + west) → **no single frontier dominates**.

## Step 4 — Original Samur solution

The specific configuration — the **Oren watershed** (the spine) + the **Sareth west wall** + the **Khor north steppe** + the **Veth south coast** + the **Tarn east periphery** — is the **Samur-specific answer** to the **Samur-specific pressures**: a large, fertile river core that is **horse-poor, timber-poor, and silver-poor**, and is **surrounded by three different kinds of frontier** (steppe, mountain, sea). It is **not a reskin**: the **spatial arrangement** (a west wall + a north steppe + a south coast, with the core behind the wall on the river) is **structurally different** from any single model, even though it produces the **same structural pressures** (multi-front, resource-scarcity, defensible-core/projection, tributary-periphery, the "too big" neighbor, maritime access).

**Load-bearing structural difference (the drift check):** the Samur's scarce resources are on **several different borders** (horses north; timber/iron west; silver north + west), so the empire must **project in several directions** — no single frontier dominates. This differs from the **Mughal** (where the south + the west dominate) and the **Qing** (where the steppe north dominates). → **not a renamed counterpart.**

## Step 5 — Consequences

- **Immediate:** the **three-front fiscal-military condition**; the **dry-season campaign / wet-season logistics** calendar; the **delta + the Veth coast** as the trade zone; the **Oren bār core** as the fiscal base.
- **Unintended:** the **standing overstretch** (holding three fronts); the **tributary-east fragmentation** (the Tarn plain); the **monetization chain** (silver → cash → the peasant / the moneylender / revolt); the **Vethra containment drain**; the **Phre's delta-mouth power-base** (the reverse-invasion axis).
- **Long-term:** the **three-front condition** is a **root cause** of the **fiscal-military overstretch** (Phase 5); the **tributary-east** is the **post-imperial fragmentation site**; the **monetization chain** is the **social fault line**; the **Vethra + the Phre** are the **two external pressures** the decline interacts with.
