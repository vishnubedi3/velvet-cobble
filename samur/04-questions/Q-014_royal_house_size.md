# Q-014 Size and composition of the royal house

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-02 §5 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-01 (§4, §5); the succession contest; DYN-02 (§5)
Why it mattered: the number of royal agnates (claimants) at any time sets the size of the succession contest.
**Resolution (now canon, DYN-02 §5):** the royal house (the Samur agnates) is ~**10–30 senior agnates** at any time (the founding Samur warrior band's kin, plus the absorbed royal-line marriages) — enough for a real contest (the "eldest vs. the most worthy" + the three-way), but not so many that the succession is unmanageable. The **military houses** are ~**5–10 founding-family military houses** (the top commanders, the Shreshthas, the frontier generals).
