# Q-001 The Sareth passes

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01 (§3); will constrain GEO-02 (cities at the passes), MIL-01 (frontier defense), ECO-01 (the timber/iron route)
Why it matters: the passes are the only viable west routes; their number, names, and relative difficulty determine where the empire's western defense is stationed, where the western trade towns sit, and how costly iron/timber projection is.
Current best guesses: 3–5 main passes (a Maratha-style chokepoint count); one dominant pass (the main invasion/trade route) with the others seasonal or for mules only.
