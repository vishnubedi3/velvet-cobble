# Q-032 Specific wars and battles

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: MIL-01 (§6, §8); constrains TIM-02+ (the wars), FOR-01 (the frontier)
Why it matters: the specific wars and battles (the Vethra wars, the Khor campaigns, the Sareth defense, the delta defense — the dates, the commanders, the outcomes) are the spine of the military history and the TIM-02+ timeline — the Vethra wars (the "state too strong" rivalry) and the Khor campaigns (the horse trade) are the two recurring conflicts.
Current best guesses: the **Vethra wars** (the "state too strong" rivalry — the first war ~KE 200–240, TIM-01; recurring in the High Empire + the Stress), the **Khor campaigns** (the horse trade — the standing northern frontier), the **Sareth defense** (the passes — the western defense), the **delta defense** (the Phre — the maritime, the Fragmentation) — the **specific battles** are to be fixed (TIM-02+).
