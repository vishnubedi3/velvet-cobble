# Religious Systems as Historical Institutions

## Mandate

Study **Sanatan traditions, Islam, Judaism, and Christianity** as historical systems: theology, institutions, political relationships, internal disputes. Not as aesthetic palettes or moral positions.

## Why (drift control)

In Samur canon, a religion is an institution with: a **material base** (land, patronage, tithes, trade), **internal factions** (orthodox/revivalist/sectarian; clerical/courtly), a **relationship to the state** (accommodation, competition, symbiosis), and a **history of disputes** that constrains future policy options. Transplanting a real religion "as vibes" is drift and is prohibited. The Samur religious landscape (Phase 4) will be designed using these systems as a *mechanism library*, with the influence register recording which mechanism came from which model.
