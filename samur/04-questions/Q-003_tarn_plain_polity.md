# Q-003 The political structure of the Tarn plain

Status: RESOLVED → ADM-02 §3 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the Tarn plain's political structure (a single tributary polity, or several local elites)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the Tarn was fixed as "held by tribute" (ADM-01 §2) but not structured (how many elites, what mechanism).
- Evidence/reasoning: the Maratha chauth mechanism (tribute without administration) + the ADM-01 §2 tiered control + the DYN-01 §2 hereditary-in-practice chiefs; the Tarn is remote + water-crossed (GEO-01 §6) so direct administration is uneconomical (NS-01 integration cost).
- Alternatives considered: a single tributary polity (rejected — the plain is several territories, not one; the ADM-01 §7 chief evasion is a multi-chief phenomenon); direct administration (rejected — the integration cost, the NS-01).
- Selected resolution: a **tribute confederation of 3 houses** (the Keshath/Taren/Orenam, the DYN-03 §3) under the **Tarnesh fourth** (1/4 of the net pell harvest + the crossings' tolls; 1/2 remitted, 1/2 retained) + a **nominal Tarn governor** (the Tarenam, non-enforcing) + the **Tarenam head temple** (the 7th Veshna regional head temple, the REL-02 §4).
- Why chosen: it is the ADM-01 §2 "held by tribute" made operational, with the evasion mechanism (the 1/2 retention) as the TIM-02 "Tarn tribute crisis KE 490" cause.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: ADM-02 §3, DYN-03 §3, TIM-03 §6 (the Tarn civil war), the ADM-01 §7 chief evasion.
- Canon status: CANON (ADM-02 §3).
- Relevant research: the comparative `maratha.md` (the chauth), the `vijayanagara.md` (the chief system).
- Recovery point: recovery/pre-question-resolutions (e48d9a3).
