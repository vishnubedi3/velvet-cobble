# Q-007 Ethnic and linguistic composition of the regions

Status: RESOLVED → CUL-01 §2 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01, GEO-02; CUL-01 (§2); DYN-01 (the legitimacy), FOR-01 (the neighbors)
Why it mattered: the empire's regions are different in kind (the bār core, the pell coast, the tribute east, the steppe, the coast) — so they are different in people and language. This shaped the legitimacy claim, the administrative language, and the fault lines of the later fragmentation.
**Resolution (now canon, CUL-01 §2):** the empire is a **multi-lingual empire** with a **dominant administrative language** — the **Samur language** (the core/administrative — the court, the bureaucracy, the Veshna commentarial tradition) + the **coastal trade pidgin** (the delta/Voren) + the **Khor language** (the steppe, the north) + the **Tarn language(s)** (the tributary east) + the **Veth language** (the Vethra, the south) + the **Phre language** (the maritime, the delta). The Samur language has **regional dialects** (the core, the middle-Oren, the Sareth, the delta). The specific Tarn/Veth/Phre languages are Q-042/Q-043/Q-044.
