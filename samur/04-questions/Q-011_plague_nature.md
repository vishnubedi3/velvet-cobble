# Q-011 The nature of the "plague" epidemic

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DEM-01 (§4); will constrain TIM (the epidemic events), CUL-01 (the historical memory)
Why it matters: the epidemic is a once-in-a-generation shock that can break a dynasty's legitimacy and empty the fields; its *nature* (a single named disease vs. a range) shapes the historical memory and the "plague" events on the timeline.
Current best guesses: a **range** of epidemic diseases (a "plague" as a category, not a single pathogen) — the dense core + the Oren/sea highway makes the empire vulnerable to **traveling epidemics** (a pre-industrial pattern); the specific named "plague" events will be dated on the TIM timeline as they arise.
