# Q-036 The specific Veshna theology

Status: RESOLVED → REL-02 §1/§2 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the specific Veshna theology (the dharma grammar, the commentarial tradition, the texts, the ritual)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the REL-01 §1 fixed the "centralized dharma" but not the corpus/theology.
- Evidence/reasoning: the Sanatan dharma grammar (the context-dependent dharma, the karma, the moksha, the varna/jati) + the REL-01 §1 "the centralized dharma."
- Alternatives considered: a creed (rejected — the REL-01 §1 "not a creed, a grammar"); a single text (rejected — the corpus is the 4 parts, the REL-02 §1).
- Selected resolution: the **sacred corpus** — the Kesra Charter (the Kesra-Veshna, the short foundational text, the oldest text, the Veshna sacred script), the Veshna Shastras (the matha's dharma-shastra commentary cycle, ~60–80 works across KE 40–500), the Rites (the consecration rite, the festival rites tied to the wind calendar), the Hagiography (the Kesra legend, the founding myth). The **theology** — the dharma by station (the king's/priest's/warrior's/merchant's/peasant's dharma, context-dependent), the karma (the moral causation across lives, the social glue, the critique target), the moksha (the liberation, the priestly telos; the lay telos = the dharma-fulfillment + the good death, the two-tier telos), the varna framework (the ideal frame, contested from within, the jati is the operative unit).
- Why chosen: it is the Sanatan dharma grammar + the REL-01 §1 centralized dharma made nominally/corpusly operationally operational, with the two-tier telos as the REL-02 §2 internal fault line.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the REL-02 §1/§2, the CUL-02 §2 (the Veshna sacred script), the Q-078 (the Kesra Charter's full text).
- Canon status: CANON (REL-02 §1/§2).
