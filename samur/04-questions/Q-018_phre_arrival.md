# Q-018 The Phre's arrival — form and KE year

Status: RESOLVED → FOR-01 §4.1 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-01 (§3, §4), GEO-01 (§8.6); FOR-01 (§4); the Fragmentation
Why it mattered: the Phre (the distant maritime power, the chartered-company logic) is a *completing* trigger — its exact form (the chartered company's structure) and its first delta enclave (the KE year) determine how it captures Voren and adds the reverse-invasion axis.
**Resolution (now canon, FOR-01 §4.1):** the Phre is a **distant maritime power** (the island, the British logic) that projects through a **chartered trading company** (the private monopoly with the right to wage war, make treaties, and collect revenue — the British EIC logic). The **chartered company** is the Phre's **state-within-a-state** (the externalized cost of empire). The Phre establishes its **first enclave at Voren** (the delta mouth, **~KE 500–560**, TIM-01) — the **economic center of gravity** (GEO-02) and the **reverse-invasion axis** (GEO-01 §2). The **specific history** (the dates, the leaders, the outcomes) is Q-048.
