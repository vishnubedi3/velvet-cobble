# Q-017 The dynastic list of the House of Kesra

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-02 §2 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-01 (§3), DYN-01 (§4, §5); the succession crises; DYN-02 (§2)
Why it mattered: the individual kings (their KE dates, names, and the character of each reign) are the spine of the timeline and the succession crises.
**Resolution (now canon, DYN-02 §2):** **14 named kings** over the ~680 years of the House (KE 0–680), average reign ~48 years — **Kesra I** (the Founder, 0–35), **Kesra II** (the Consolidator, 35–72), **Veshna I** (the Expander, 72–120), **Sharan I** (the Frontier King, 120–175), **Kharan I** (the First Vethra-War King, 175–225), **Dharan I** (the Great-Builder King, 225–290), **Varan I** (the Peak Emperor, 290–345), **Peshan I** (the Overstretch King, 345–385), **Ghoran I** (the Revolt King, 385–430), **Zharan I** (the Famine/Epidemic King, 430–480), **Maren I** (the Weak King, 480–520), **Kesra III** (the Contested King, the "eldest," the Tarn east, 525–590) + **Threna I** (the Divided King, the "most worthy," the bār core, 525–650) [concurrent], **Neshan I** (the Last King, 650–680).
