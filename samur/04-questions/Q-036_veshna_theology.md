# Q-036 The specific Veshna theology

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: REL-01 (§1); constrains the later REL files, CUL-01 (the literature, the texts)
Why it matters: the specific Veshna theology (the dharma grammar, the commentarial tradition, the texts, the ritual) defines the founding faith's content and the priestly class's knowledge monopoly — the commentarial tradition is the priestly class's self-reproduction mechanism (the parallel elite, REL-01 §2).
Current best guesses: a **dharma grammar** (the context-dependent dharma, the karma, the moksha, the varna framework) + a **commentarial tradition** (the learned in the dharma, the guru–śiṣya lineage) + a **sacred text/corpus** (the Veshna scripture — the fixed text + the derived commentary) + the **ritual** (the consecration, the festival year) — the **specific content** is to be fixed (CUL-01).
