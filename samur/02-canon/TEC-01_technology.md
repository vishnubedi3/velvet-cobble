> TEC-01 is the **technology** as a **cumulative historical process** (the Phase 4 requirement) — each technology has its **origins**, **required resources**, **adoption resistance**, and **socio-economic impact**. The technologies **accumulate** over the eras. The specific works, workshops, and diffusion are the later TEC files.

## 1. Technology as a cumulative historical process

The Samur technology is **not an aesthetic** — it is a **cumulative historical process** (the Mysore logic — the state workshops, the craft-guilds, the **dual demand** [the military + the court]). The technologies **accumulate** over the eras:

- **The Founding (0–60):** the **irrigation** (the bār core — the state-directed major works), the **basic metallurgy** (the iron, the tools).
- **The Expansion (60–220):** the **irrigation** (the Oren canals), the **metallurgy** (the steel, the Sareth/southern highlands), the **river logistics** (the Oren — the boats).
- **The High Empire (220–380):** the **artillery** (the state workshops, the guns/rockets, the military demand), the **silk/textiles/paper** (the craft-guilds, the court demand), the **metallurgy** (the steel — the state workshops).
- **The Stress (380–500):** the **artillery** (the guns for the frontier — the military demand, the overstretch), the **irrigation** (the state direction, the corvée, the resentment), the **river/sea logistics** (the Oren, the delta — the Phre's approach).
- **The Fragmentation (500–600+):** the **artillery** (the guns for the frontier — the modernization), the **river/sea logistics** (the delta — the Phre's capture of Voren), the **silk/textiles/paper** (the craft-guilds — the trade, the Phre).

## 2. The irrigation (the tanks/anicut, the Oren canals)

- **Origins:** the **bār core** (the dry-summer cereal needs irrigation) — the **state-directed** major works (the great tanks, the Oren canals), the **village** minor works (DEM-01 §5, ADM-01 §6).
- **Required resources:** the **labor** (the corvée, the agrarian surplus), the **timber** (the Sareth forests — the waterwheels, the sluices), the **stone** (the tanks).
- **Adoption resistance:** the **corvée** (the forced labor — the resentment, DEM-01 §5), the **village** (the minor works — the local control), the **temples** (the temple economy — the parallel irrigation, the temple lands).
- **Socio-economic impact:** the **surplus base** (the bār core — the fiscal base, ECO-01 §1), the **wind dependency** (the pell — the volatile base, DEM-01), the **state power** (the state directs the major works — the state's physical power, ADM-01 §6).

## 3. The metallurgy (the steel, the iron)

- **Origins:** the **Sareth foothills** + the **southern highlands** (the iron ore, GEO-01 §5) — the **state workshops** (the karkhanas, the military demand, ECO-01 §6).
- **Required resources:** the **iron ore** (the Sareth/southern highlands), the **timber/charcoal** (the Sareth forests — the smelting), the **labor** (the smiths, the craft guilds).
- **Adoption resistance:** the **craft guilds** (the smiths — the guild control, the monopoly), the **frontier** (the iron ore is beyond the Sareth — the projection cost), the **state** (the state workshops vs. the guilds).
- **Socio-economic impact:** the **military** (the steel, the guns — the MIL-01 artillery), the **craft** (the steel — the high-value craft, the court demand), the **state power** (the state workshops — the state's control of the steel).

## 4. The artillery (the guns, the rockets)

- **Origins:** the **state workshops** (the karkhanas, the military demand, ECO-01 §6, Q-033) — the **Mysore** logic (the military modernization, the guns/rockets).
- **Required resources:** the **steel/bronze** (the metallurgy), the **gunpowder** (the sulfur, the saltpeter, the charcoal — the frontier), the **labor** (the gunsmiths, the craft guilds).
- **Adoption resistance:** the **cavalry** (the Khor horses — the mobile striking force — the artillery is slow, the infantry is stationary), the **garrison** (the Sareth/Khor — the garrisons are small, the artillery is heavy), the **state** (the state workshops vs. the guilds).
- **Socio-economic impact:** the **military** (the artillery — the modernizing force, the MIL-01), the **state power** (the state workshops — the state's control of the guns), the **frontier** (the guns for the frontier — the military demand).

## 5. The silk/textiles/paper (the craft-guilds)

- **Origins:** the **craft guilds** (the silk, the steel, the textiles, the paper, ECO-01 §6) — the **Mysore** logic (the craft-guild urban economy, the court demand).
- **Required resources:** the **silk** (the sericulture, the Veth/south), the **cotton** (the bār/pell), the **paper** (the bark, the Sareth forests), the **labor** (the weavers, the guilds).
- **Adoption resistance:** the **guilds** (the craft guilds — the guild control, the monopoly), the **state** (the state workshops vs. the guilds), the **court** (the court demand — the state demand).
- **Socio-economic impact:** the **trade** (the textiles — the export, the ECO-01), the **court** (the court demand — the market anchor, the ECO-01 §6), the **craft** (the high-value craft, the urban economy).

## 6. The river/sea logistics (the Oren, the delta, the ships)

- **Origins:** the **Oren** (the logistics spine, GEO-01 §2) + the **delta** (the maritime, the Voren) — the **river/sea** transport.
- **Required resources:** the **timber** (the ships — the Sareth forests), the **labor** (the shipwrights, the sailors, the marines), the **delta** (the ports, the warehouses).
- **Adoption resistance:** the **delta** (the port officials — the transit/market dues, the ADM-01 §7), the **Phre** (the maritime — the Phre's capture of the delta, the FOR-01), the **state** (the state control of the Oren).
- **Socio-economic impact:** the **trade** (the Oren — the internal trade, the ECO-01 §3), the **logistics** (the Oren — the military logistics, the MIL-01 §4), the **delta** (the Voren — the maritime hub, the GEO-02).

## 7. The cumulative process and the dual demand

The technologies **accumulate** over the eras (the irrigation → the metallurgy → the artillery → the craft → the logistics), driven by the **dual demand** (the military + the court, the Mysore logic):

- **The military demand** — the **steel**, the **guns**, the **rockets**, the **ships** (the MIL-01, the frontier, the overstretch).
- **The court demand** — the **silk**, the **textiles**, the **paper**, the **display** (the ECO-01 §6, the market anchor).

The **dual demand** is the **load-bearing** feature (the Mysore logic) — the technology is driven by the **military** (the frontier, the overstretch) + the **court** (the market anchor, the display).

## Consequences (immediate / unintended / long-term)

- **Immediate:** the technology as a **cumulative historical process** (the irrigation → the metallurgy → the artillery → the craft → the logistics, over the eras); each technology's **origins**, **required resources**, **adoption resistance**, and **socio-economic impact**; the **dual demand** (the military + the court).
- **Unintended:** the **adoption resistance** (the corvée, the guilds, the frontier, the Phre) — the technology is **contested** (the state vs. the guilds, the state vs. the frontier, the state vs. the Phre); the **dual demand** (the military + the court) — the technology is driven by the **overstretch** (the military) + the **market anchor** (the court).
- **Long-term:** the **technology** (the irrigation, the metallurgy, the artillery, the craft, the logistics) is the **material base** of the empire's power (the state power, the military, the trade) — and its **adoption resistance** (the corvée, the guilds, the frontier, the Phre) is a **root cause** of the empire's stress and fragmentation (TIM-01).
