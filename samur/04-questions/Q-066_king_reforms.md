# Q-066 The specific reforms of each king

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: DYN-02 (§2), TIM-02; constrains TIM-03+, the later ADM/REL files
Why it matters: the specific reforms of each king (the individual reforms, their KE dates, their outcomes) are the "decisions made in one generation that constrain the next" (the Phase 5 causality-tracing requirement).
Current best guesses: the **Kesra I** reforms (the dual structure, the chief system, the revenue), the **Dharan I** reforms (the great building, the irrigation), the **Peshan I** reforms (the overstretch, the frontier), the **Ghoran I** reforms (the revolt, the monetization), the **Maren I** reforms (the Shreshtha's dominance) — the **specific reforms** are to be fixed (TIM-03+).
