# Q-015 The initial division of the absorbed chiefs' districts

Status: RESOLVED → DYN-03 §5 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Original question: the initial division of the absorbed chiefs' districts (how many chiefs, how large are the districts)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the DYN-01 §2 fixed the chief system but not the district division.
- Evidence/reasoning: the Vijayanagara nayaka mechanism (the chief's district) + the DYN-01 §2 "the chiefs keep their districts" + the Dharan I program (the 7 Grand Districts, the TIM-03 §4).
- Alternatives considered: a single large chief district (rejected — the Tarn is 3 houses, the bār is ~40 minor); a flat village system (rejected — the ADM-02 §1 "the clerks collect" needs the district level).
- Selected resolution: the **High-Empire standard** — the bār core = the 7 Grand Districts (the canal/tank districts, the Dharan I program) + the ~40 minor districts; the Tarn = the 3 house territories + the delta fringe; the Sareth = the 2 garrison districts + the pass villages; the Khor frontier = the Keshkhor district + the mobile-cavalry district; the coast = the Voren district + the Kesveth district. The chiefs' districts are **hereditary-in-practice** (the DYN-01 §2).
- Why chosen: it is the DYN-01 §2 chief system + the Dharan I program made districtally operational.
- Systems affected / new dependencies: the DYN-03 §5, the ADM-02 §3 (the Tarn), the TIM-03 §4 (the 7 Grand Districts).
- Canon status: CANON (DYN-03 §5).
- Relevant research: the comparative `vijayanagara.md` (the nayaka district).
- Recovery point: recovery/pre-question-resolutions (e48d9a3).
- Resulting changes: DYN-03 §5 (new), the influence register (the DYN-03 row), the CHANGELOG.
