# Q-047 The specific Khor structure

Status: RESOLVED → FOR-02 §3 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: FOR-01 (§3), TIM-02, FOR-02 (§3); the Khor campaigns
Why it mattered: the specific Khor structure (the horse peoples — the number, the names, the seasonal assembly, the leaders) defines the steppe's political structure and the horse trade.
**Resolution (now canon, FOR-02 §3):** the Khor's assembly leaders (the seasonal assembly's leaders) — **Khesh Ur** (the First Assembly Leader, ~100–180, the founding of the seasonal assembly), **Ghoran Ur** (the Trade Leader, ~180–300, the horse trade), **Zhan Ur** (the Frontier Leader, ~300–450, the hard frontier), **Maren Ur** (the Stress Leader, ~450–550, the Khor's Stress), **Khesh Ur II** (the Fragmentation Leader, ~550–680, the Khor's Fragmentation). The seasonal assembly is **re-elected** (the loose confederation, not hereditary). The specific Khor horse peoples are Q-072.
