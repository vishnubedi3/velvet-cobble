# Transformation Log — MIL-01 (The Military)

Date: 2026-08-26
Canon target: **MIL-01** (the military as a political/economic institution, the layered recruitment, the arms, the logistics, the war finance, the specialist forces by border, the long-term consequences of overstretch)
Method: the 5-step sequential reasoning.

## Step 1 — Historical examples and underlying mechanisms

- **Mughal** — the **gunpowder army of the mansabdars** (the fixed cavalry quotas + the artillery + the infantry), the army as an extension of the **fiscal-mansabdari** system, the **war finance** (the revenue surplus + the state trade + the imperial borrowing), the **Deccan overstretch** (the conquest without the capacity to absorb).
- **Maratha** — the **light cavalry** (the shiledar, from the agrarian-military castes), the **gupta/dash irregulars** (the raiding, the foraging, the harassing), the **professionalization** (the artillery, the fort), the **mule logistics**, the **seasonal campaigns**, the **irregulars that cannot be stood down**, the **supply-line failure** (Panipat).
- **Vijayanagara** — the **war elephants** (the Deccan signature), the cavalry, the **artillery** (after 1565), the siege of the walled hill cities, the **frontier warfare**.
- **Mysore** — the **military modernization** (the European-style infantry, the artillery, the rockets), the **fortification**, the **frontier state** with the modernized military.
- **Qing** — the **banners** (the conqueror's military-kinship corps), the **frontier garrisons**, the **steppe frontier** (defensible only by presence).
- **British** — the **fiscal-military state**, the **naval logistics**, the **sepoy army** (the company's army + the native intermediaries).

## Step 2 — Original historical conditions

- The Mughal gunpowder army + the fiscal-mansabdari + the war finance + the Deccan overstretch.
- The Maratha light cavalry + the gupta/dash + the professionalization + the mule logistics + the seasonal campaigns + the supply-line failure.
- The Vijayanagara war elephants + the cavalry + the artillery + the frontier warfare.
- The Mysore military modernization + the fortification + the frontier state.
- The Qing banners + the frontier garrisons + the steppe frontier.
- The British fiscal-military state + the naval logistics + the sepoy army.

## Step 3 — Equivalent or contrasting Samur conditions

- The Samur military is a **political and economic institution** (not just a fighting force) — the state's chief instrument of power, its chief employer, and its chief driver of the economy.
- The Samur recruitment is **layered** (the peasant-soldier base from the dense core, the chiefs' levies, the border elite, the founding elite/military houses).
- The Samur arms are the **infantry (the base) + the cavalry (the Khor-dependent striking force) + the war elephants (the Sareth/southern specialty) + the artillery (the state workshops) + the irregulars (the gupta/dash) + the marines (the delta)**.
- The Samur logistics are **seasonal** (the dry-season campaign, the wet-season river logistics) + the **Oren spine** + the **Khor horse dependency** + the **mule/ox** + the **supply-line vulnerability**.
- The Samur war finance is a **mix** of the **revenue surplus + the shahukar debt + the state monopoly** — the **shahukar debt** creates the **growth imperative**.
- The Samur specialist forces are **by border** (the Sareth garrison, the Khor cavalry, the delta marines, the Tarn levies, the core field army).

**Contrasts (the load-bearing differences):** the **Khor horse dependency** (the cavalry depends on the Khor — a strategic vulnerability, unlike the Mughal where the horses are more internal); the **war elephants as a specialty** (the Sareth/southern, the Vijayanagara logic, but the **load-bearing** force is the **cavalry** (the Khor), not the elephants); the **dual-demand** (the military demand drives the metallurgy, the ECO-01 logic); the **layered recruitment** (the four layers: the peasant base, the chiefs' levies, the border elite, the military houses).

## Step 4 — Original Samur solution

The **Samur military** — a political/economic institution; the layered recruitment (the peasant base, the chiefs' levies, the border elite, the military houses); the arms (the infantry, the cavalry, the elephants, the artillery, the irregulars, the marines); the logistics (the season, the Oren, the Khor dependency, the mule/ox, the supply line); the war finance (the shahukar, the growth imperative, the revenue surplus, the state monopoly); the specialist forces (the Sareth garrison, the Khor cavalry, the delta marines, the Tarn levies, the core field army) — is the **Samur-specific answer** to the **Samur conditions** (the Oren spine, the Sareth wall, the Khor steppe, the Veth coast, the Tarn plain; the horse-poor core; the frontier silver; the three-front condition; the dual elite). It is **not a reskin**: the **Khor horse dependency** (a strategic vulnerability), the **war elephants as a specialty** (the load-bearing force is the cavalry, the Khor), the **dual-demand** (the military demand drives the metallurgy), and the **layered recruitment** (the four layers) are the load-bearing differences.

## Step 5 — Consequences

- **Immediate:** the military as a political/economic institution; the layered recruitment; the arms; the logistics; the war finance; the specialist forces; the military as a political institution.
- **Unintended:** the Khor horse dependency (a strategic vulnerability); the growth imperative (the overstretch); the irregulars that cannot be stood down (the standing problem); the supply-line failure (the vulnerability); the conquest without integration (the fiscal-military death spiral).
- **Long-term:** the three-front overstretch, the fiscal-military tension, the growth imperative, the conquest without integration, the irregulars that cannot be stood down, the supply-line failure — all **root causes** of the Phase 5 decline (TIM-01).
