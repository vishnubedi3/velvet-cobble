# Q-034 The specific cavalry (the Khor horses)

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: MIL-01 (§3, §4); constrains FOR-01 (the Khor), the horse trade
Why it matters: the specific cavalry (the Khor horses — the number, the quality, the dependency, the horse-trade terms) defines the strategic vulnerability (a failed Khor = no horses = no cavalry, MIL-01 §4) — the Khor horse trade is a strategic lifeline (the cavalry depends on the Khor, GEO-01 §5, ECO-01 §3).
Current best guesses: the **Khor horses** (the steppe horses — the quality, the number) — the **cavalry** (the Samur riders on the Khor horses) of ~20–40k in the High Empire; the **horse trade** (the Khor horse-people — the terms, the price, the dependency) — the **specific numbers** and the **trade terms** are to be fixed (FOR-01, the Khor).
