## The invariants (the world's load-bearing constants)

1. **The wind law (GEO-03):** the sea-wind (the wet season, months 1–6) + the land-wind (the dry season, months 7–12); the regime: strong (~5–7 years), weak (~3–5 years), failed (~7–12 years), great failure (~80–120 years). The bār core is stable **because of the irrigation buffer** (the Oren Grand Canal + the 7 Grand Tanks); the pell coast is volatile; the Khor steppe is independent (the bad-grass year ~7–10 years). **The failed wind → the pell fails → the famine → the debt → the revolt.**
2. **The Oren spine (GEO-01):** the great river (the Oren + the Khel + the Tarn) is the logistics spine, the revenue route, and the reverse-invasion axis. **The delta mouth (the Voren) is the chokepoint** — whoever holds it holds the trade.
3. **The three-front condition (GEO-01 §8.1):** the Sareth (the west wall, the iron/timber), the Khor (the north steppe, the horses), the coast (the Veth/Phre, the pepper/silver) — **no single frontier dominates**; the empire must hold all three simultaneously (the standing overstretch).
4. **The dual structure (DYN-01 §3):** the **King** (the legitimacy: the heartland, the temple, the protector of dharma) + the **Shreshtha** (the power: the army, the revenue, the administration) + the **Veshna matha** (the sanctioner: the consecration, the de-legitimation, the kingmaker). The **dual elite** (the founding military houses vs. the absorbed clerk class) runs the bureaucracy (the ADM-02 §1).
5. **The monetization chain (GEO-01 §8.4, ECO-02 §1):** the frontier silver (the Khor + the Sareth; from KE 500 the Phre's island silver) → the silver coin (the Kesra standard, the 90% fine) → the assessed-in-silver tax (the 1/4 bār, the +1/8 Dhresh duty) → the cash crops (the indigo coercion) → the moneylender (the shahukar) → the peasant debt → **the revolt when the targets inflate** (the KE 400–408 bār Revolt).
6. **The name pool (CUL-01, DYN-02 §1):** the consonant-rich Samur register (the -a/-an/-esh/-ath suffixes) is the canon name pool — all names come from it. The scripts: the Orenic (the king's law), the Veshna sacred (the frozen orthography, the matha's authority), the sea-script (the Phre), the knot-tally (the Khor). The languages: the Orenic family (the Samur/Veth/Tarn), the Khoric, the Phreic, the delta pidgin.

## The causal chains (the world's load-bearing causality)

- **The founding → the empire:** the chokepoint + the fiscal base (the DYN-01 §1) → the absorption of the chiefs (the hereditary-in-practice districts) + the co-option of the Veshna (the centralized dharma) → the dual structure → the High-Empire (the Dharan I program: the canal, the tanks, the temple, the karkhanas).
- **The wind → the decline:** the great failure (the KE 455–460) → the Great Famine (the KE 455–470) → the refugee flow → the Great Epidemic (the KE 460–480, the compound) → the ~10–15% contraction → the labor collapse → the fiscal base breaks → the Stress.
- **The Khor → the cavalry → the strategy:** the horse-poor core (the GEO-01 §5) → the Khor horse trade (the 2–4k/year, the Keshkhor) → the cavalry (the 8–12k, the shiledar) → the strategic vulnerability (a Khor bad-grass year / the crisis → the cavalry shortfall).
- **The Phre → the delta → the sea-lanes:** the Phre's arrival (the KE 500 contact, the KE 510 First Enclave — during the Stress) → the consolidation (the KE 530 Second Enclave, the KE 543 Voren Mouth — during the Fragmentation) → the sea-lane control → the Phre silver (a lifeline + a leash) → the "completing trigger" (not the causal root).
- **The Tarnesh Dawn → the matha split → the crisis:** the revival (the KE 405–460) → the matha split (the Tarn wing vs. the Veshna orthodoxy wing) → the withheld sanction (the KE 525, the "de-consecration in absentia") → the Great Succession Crisis (the KE 520–555) → the division (the KE 528) → the successor states.

## The structures (the world's load-bearing institutions)

- **The 7 regions** (GEO-02): the Samur (the heartland), the Middle Oren (the bār core), the Sareth Marches (the west wall), the Khor Frontier (the north), the Tarn Marches (the tribute east), the Oren Delta (the port zone), the Veth Shore (the coast). The control: the direct (the bār core), the garrison (the Sareth/Khor), the tribute (the Tarn), the port (the delta/coast).
- **The 9 cities** (GEO-02, DEM-02 §2): Kesra (the capital, 150–250k), Voren (the delta port, 150–250k), Veshna (the temple city, 100–150k), Besra (the grain market, 80–120k), Threna (the western fortress, 30–50k), Keshkhor (the horse emporium, 30–50k), Tarenam (the Tarn head town, 30–50k), Kesveth (the Veth port, 20–40k), Gheshar (the waystation, 10–20k) + ~100 towns + ~30k villages.
- **The 7 offices** (ADM-02 §1): the Shreshtha, the Beshara, the Dhresh, the Guru, the Kesrath, the Vorenesh, the frontier generals.
- **The 4 laws** (ADM-02 §2): the king's law (the Beshara Code + the Dhresh Code), the jati law (the panchayat), the temple law (the Veshna priests, the apex = the matha), the port law (the Vorenesh + the port merchants, the Phre enclave law from KE 500).
- **The temple network** (REL-02 §4): the 1 Great Temple (the Veshna Great Temple) + the 7 Regional Head Temples + the ~250–400 Local Temples; the temple land ~10–15% of the arable (the tax-exempt parallel fiscal base).
- **The 3 Tarn houses** (DYN-03 §3): the Keshath, the Taren, the Orenam (the Tarnesh fourth: 1/4, 1/2 remitted).
- **The 5 military houses** (DYN-03 §1): the Gheshar (the Shreshtha's dominance), the Threnath (the Sareth Dhresh), the Keshan (the cavalry), the Dharan (the water-officers), the Zharan (the gunners).
- **The 4 Khor clans** (DYN-03 §6): the Kheshur (the assembly seat), the Ghoranur (the horse breeders), the Zhanur (the raiders), the Marenur (the traders).
- **The army** (MIL-02 §1): the standing ~35–45k, the full mobilization 80–110k, the ~0.3% standing-rate ceiling (the structural limit).
