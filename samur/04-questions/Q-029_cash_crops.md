# Q-029 The specific cash crops

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: ECO-01 (§1); constrains the later ECO files, the monetization chain
Why it matters: the specific cash crops (the indigo, the cotton, the sugarcane — which are grown where, the cash-crop coercion) define the monetization chain and the social fault line — the cash-crop coercion (the peasant must grow the cash crop to pay the silver tax) is what drives the peasant debt and the revolt (the monetization chain, TIM-01).
Current best guesses: the **indigo** (the dye, the export), the **cotton** (the textiles, the bulk), the **sugarcane** (the sugar, the local) — grown in the **bār core** (the irrigated zones) and the **pell coast** (the wet zones) — the **cash-crop coercion** (the state pushes the cash crops for the silver tax) is the **social fault line** (the revolt when the targets inflate).
