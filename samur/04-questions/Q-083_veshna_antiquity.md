# Q-083 The Veshna faith's pre-KE-0 antiquity

Status: RESOLVED → TIM-05 (2026-08-28)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: TIM-04 §2 (the pre-imperial history), REL-02 §1 (the Kesra Charter, "the oldest text... orthography is frozen"), CUL-02 §2 (the Veshna sacred script, "older" and "frozen"), DYN-01 §2 #3 (the founding co-opted the Veshna temple)
Why it mattered: the established canon demonstrates that the Veshna faith/temple pre-dated the founding (KE 0) (DYN-01 §2 #3 "the founding co-opted the Veshna temple, the founding faith's center"; REL-02 §1 "the Kesra Charter... is the oldest text (its orthography is frozen)"; CUL-02 §2 "the Veshna sacred script — an older abugida... conservative... frozen"). This is a genuine internal-causality implication of pre-imperial history. But the **scale of that pre-imperial antiquity is not specified** in the established canon.

**Resolution (now canon, TIM-05 §1/§4–§8):** the pre-imperial history is a **genuine, deep history** — the **Pre-Kesra Age**. The Veshna faith/temple is not merely "older than KE 0": it is the center of an attested pre-imperial span of **~1,200 years (~KE −1200 → KE 0)** — the scale is **derived, not assigned**, from four internal-causality anchors (the frozen sacred script's conservatism; the Orenic dialectal drift + the Tarn substrate; the temple's multi-night survival claim over every ruling house; the polities' multi-generational depth — TIM-05 §1). The faith's **formation** (the dharma grammar's crystallization at the Veshna mountain shrine, the proto-consecration's rite form, the Orenar line — the Age of the Mountain, ~KE −1150 → −800), the **temple polity** (the bār chiefdoms' league, the devanama's origin, the three standing laws, the sacred script's freezing ~KE −500, the Orenic script's emergence as its secular derivative, the daughter shrines — the Temple Period, ~KE −800 → −265), the **Long Night** (the last great failure, KE −265 → −250 — the multi-year catastrophe that breaks the old order and positions the Samur's rise), and the **Recovery** (KE −250 → 0 — the Samur's protectorate + consolidation, the temple's re-co-option, the rite's scope-extension, the founding's preparation) are **canon**. The **Kesra Charter's frozen orthography is causally explained** (the script was frozen before the Charter was written — TIM-05 §5/§7). **The full pre-imperial depth beyond the Deep-Age boundary (~KE −1200) is unattested** — the world's own record's horizon; **no number is assigned** to the unattested depth. The **Kesra Era (KE 0 to the present, the six epochs) is unchanged.** (Recovery point: recovery/pre-TIM-05.)
