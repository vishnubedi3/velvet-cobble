## 1. The era boundaries (resolves Q-016)

The **official periodization remains the TIM-01 ranges** (the **structural divisions are approximate by nature** — that is correct; the **events are exact**): the **Founding (KE 0–60)**, the **Expansion (KE 60–220)**, the **High Empire (KE 220–380)**, the **Stress (KE 380–500)**, the **Fragmentation (KE 500–600+)**. The **exact event dates** are fixed in §2–§7 (all within the ranges).
