# Q-001 The Sareth passes

Status: RESOLVED → GEO-02 §3 (2026-08-26)
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01 (§3); GEO-02 (§3); MIL-01 (frontier defense); ECO-01 (the timber/iron route)
Why it mattered: the passes are the only viable west routes; their number and difficulty determine where the western defense is stationed and how costly iron/timber projection is.
**Resolution (now canon, GEO-02 §3):** **three main passes + minor mule tracks**, one dominant — the **Thren Gate** (main invasion/trade route; fortified town **Threna** at its inner lip), the **Vesh Gate** (second pass; the timber/iron route; waystation **Gheshar**), and the **Ghul Gate** (high, seasonal military route; garrison post only). Defense and trade are **separated by pass**: an invader is funneled to the Thren Gate, the empire's own projection uses the Vesh Gate.
