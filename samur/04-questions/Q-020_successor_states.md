Status: RESOLVED → TIM-03 §7 (2026-08-26)

Original question: the specific successor states (which chiefs' districts become which states; their names, their centers, their dates)?

**Resolution record**
- Why it was unresolved: the TIM-02 fixed the fragmentation (the "successor states") but not the names/centers/dates.
- Evidence/reasoning: the TIM-02 "the successor states (the Tarn east, the Sareth west, the bār core + the Khor north)" + the ADM-02 §1 "the successor states' templates" + the DYN-03 houses.
- Alternatives considered: a single successor state (rejected — the division is 3 + the Vethra + the Phre); a many-states fragmentation (rejected — the 3 + the Vethra + the Phre is the TIM-02 fix).

The **Veshna matha** survives across all (the seat at Veshna, the recognition contested — the Q-079).
- Why chosen: it is the TIM-02 "the successor states" made nominally/centrally/datedly operational, with the ADM-02 §1 inheritance.
