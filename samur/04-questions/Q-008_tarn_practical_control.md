# Q-008 Practical control of the Tarn Marches

Status: OPEN
Date: 2026-08-26
Related: GEO-01 (§8.3), GEO-02; refines Q-003; will constrain ADM-01 (the tributary administration)
Why it matters: the Tarn Marches are the tributary periphery and the future fragmentation site; *how* the center controls them (a single tributary governor vs. several local elites) determines the revenue extraction and the shape of the successor states.
Current best guesses: **several local elites** under a **nominal tributary arrangement** (the "inner colony" pattern) rather than one consolidated tributary polity — consolidation there is a *later* development to be causally tracked (the successor state that emerges will be the one that consolidates first).
