# 09 — Evaluation

Optimize for **reliable canon preservation**, not maximum blocking.

| Kind | Meaning | Suite pressure |
|---|---|---|
| **False acceptance** | Violation of established canon, contamination, or bypass that received PASS | A01 T1, A07, A12 T0, A28–A31, A36–A37 |
| **False rejection** | Legitimate expansion or Arena-following work that was BLOCKED | A08 T0, A22, A35 |

Metrics (host-reported, not baked into the skill as scores):

- Gate catch rate on blocking fixtures
- Warning (not block) on ignore-Arena and classified conflict
- Expansion allowed when no collision
- Stale contracts refused
- Post-gen catch rate on introduced contradictions

Do not tune the guard by counting how often it blocks.
